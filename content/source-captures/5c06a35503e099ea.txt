What Is Competency-Based Education?
Designed to leverage your previous knowledge, college coursework, and work experience, competency-based education enables you to earn your degree at your pace as you demonstrate mastery of relevant academic content.
How our YourPace online programs save you time and money:
Prove your knowledge, move ahead—the faster you advance, the more you save
Flat tuition rate per 8-week session, no matter how many courses you complete
With no weekly deadlines, you control the speed of completion*
*Time to completion varies by student and is based on several factors including prior college coursework, relevant work experience, amount of time dedicated to studying, and the number of courses completed each session.

Psychology, BA
Discover our 100% online Psychology program
Gain a deeper understanding of human interaction while you prepare for career opportunities by leveraging the YourPace online Bachelor of Arts in Psychology program. Designed to instill a strong grounding in the science of human behavior and mental processes, this competency-based program also provides a solid foundation for graduate school.
Online coursework for this accredited online program sharpens your critical thinking and develops your ability to interpret, analyze, and communicate in a wide variety of professional and academic settings. You will also explore topics related to the social-services aspects of psychology, including social and cognitive psychology, lifespan and personality development, research methods, assessment, as well as drugs and behavior.
As a graduate of this YourPace online program, you will:
Examine the scientific study of behavior and mental health
Develop skills in research and analysis as it applies to behavioral science
Gain an understanding of the fundamentals and theories affecting sex, gender, personality, disability, and cognitive psychology
Explore the treatment approaches for substance abuse counseling
Take part in real-world application of the skills you have learned in a supervised field practicum
Career opportunities with a psychology degree:
Substance Abuse Counselor
Mental Health Counselor
Case Worker
Behavioral Health Specialist
Human Resources Specialist
Other roles in areas such as forensic psychology or organizational psychology
Graduates will also be prepared to advance into graduate level degree programs to prepare for more advanced roles. An undergraduate degree in Psychology can also serve as a strong foundation for a graduate degree in Social Work.

Through UMPI’s innovative YourPace format, you can progress at your own speed—leveraging prior experience and expertise while mastering new concepts to fit your career goals.
Whether you’re seeking career advancement, exploring roles in counseling, leadership, or human resources, or preparing for further education, the BAS with a Psychology Minor empowers you with versatile skills that are in high demand across industries.

Applied Bachelor of Arts in Psychology 90-credit program, BA
The Applied Bachelor of Arts in Psychology (90-Credit Program) is designed for adult degree completers returning to college after a two-year pause, with prior college credit (or credit for prior learning). The program provides foundational skills in human behavior, communication, and problem-solving, preparing graduates for entry-level roles in general business, human services, or administrative roles where people skills are highly valued.
Important Program Considerations & Advising Because this is a 90-credit degree rather than a traditional 120-credit bachelor’s degree, applicants and prospective students should consider the following possible academic and career factors prior to enrollment:
Graduate School & Licensure: Requirements for advanced education, state licensing, and professional certifications vary widely by institution and state. Many graduate programs, state licensing boards, and credentialing bodies may require a standard 120-credit baccalaureate degree for admission or eligibility. Students are strongly encouraged to thoroughly research the specific entry requirements for any graduate program, license, or career field they plan to pursue before selecting this degree option. Please note that this program does not lead directly to clinical psychology licensure or counseling credentials.
Employer Recognition: Certain employers or federal/state agencies may require a standard 120-credit degree for specific job classifications or promotional pathways.
Transition to a 120-Credit Degree: Students enrolled in the 90-credit program may choose to transition to UMPI’s traditional 120-credit Bachelor of Arts in Psychology (or another 120-credit major) at any time. Coursework completed in the 90-credit track applies directly toward the 120-credit degree without loss of credit.
Financial Aid Notice: Federal financial aid regulations may limit or restrict additional financial aid eligibility for students who complete a 90-credit degree and subsequently re-enroll to earn a 120-credit degree.
Once you are admitted to the Psychology program and your Transfer Credit Report is finalized, your Academic Success Coach will reach out to you directly by email to explain next steps in the enrollment process. At that time, you can request to meet with your Success Coach to review your eligibility for the 90-credit program and confirm the degree pathway that best aligns with your educational and career goals.

Applied Bachelor of Arts in Psychology 90-credit program, B.A.
The Applied B.A. in Psychology at the University of Maine at Presque Isle is a 90-credit degree program designed to provide students with a foundational understanding of psychological principles and their practical application across a variety of professional settings. The program emphasizes the development of critical thinking, communication, problem-solving, and data analysis skills essential for careers in mental health services, education, business, and human resources.
Through a combination of academic coursework, experiential learning, and opportunities for research and internships, students gain practical experience and preparation for graduate study or entry into the workforce. The streamlined curriculum allows for flexibility and is well-suited for transfer students and working adults seeking a focused path to degree completion. Graduates will be equipped to apply psychological knowledge in diverse, real-world contexts and to make meaningful contributions in their chosen fields.
This program includes 40 credit hours of general education courses, 45 credit hours of Psychology/core courses, and a minimum of 5 credit hours of electives. Of these credits, at least 30 credits, including substantial advanced work in the major, must be awarded by UMPI to meet the graduation residency requirement.
Transparency Disclosure
As its name indicates, the Applied B.A. in Psychology is a 90-credit program, not a traditional 120-credit program. Some employers and graduate programs may not accept applicants or candidates with an undergraduate degree of fewer than 120 credits. Students are encouraged to speak with an academic advisor at UMPI about this 90-credit program before enrolling. In the event that students enrolled in Applied Bachelor of Arts in Psychology 90-credit program decide to pursue the university’s Bachelor of Arts in Psychology program, students will be able to complete the change of major process, and the university will provide a no-loss-of-credit pathway from the 90-credit to the 120-credit version of the program. Alternatively, students may transfer into a different 120-credit degree program for which they qualify academically. Federal financial aid may not be available for students who earn a 90-credit degree and then return to complete a 120-credit degree program.
