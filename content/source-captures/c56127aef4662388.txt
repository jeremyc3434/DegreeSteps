BS in Information Technology, Network and
Cloud Computing
School of Business, Technology, and Health Care
Administration
3 The Network and Cloud Computing specialization is
designed to provide students with the knowledge and
3 skills needed to administer networks in a variety of
environments. The curriculum addresses planning,
3 designing, configuring, and troubleshooting networks
3 in environments ranging from small, local networks to
3 enterprise-wide integrated cloud infrastructures.
Specific topics include cloud migration, cloud and
3 server administration, internetworking architecture
3 design, Linux administration, artificial intelligence and
machine learning, and system assurance and security.
3 Upon successful completion of this specialization,
students are prepared to pursue careers as cloud
administrator, cloud engineer, cloud architect,
network analyst, network administrator, and network
engineer.
