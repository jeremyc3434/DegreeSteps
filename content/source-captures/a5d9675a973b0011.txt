MKT-4790 Applied Marketing Practices
This course is designed to bring together both the marketing theory students have encountered and practical experience students have from their own work-related experiences. Students will learn to apply knowledge and experience they already have to "real-world" situations. During this course students will have the opportunity to develop a complete marketing plan for a new business (Marketing Plan) and students can then use this work as a portfolio of their work for current and prospective employers.
The critical themes presented in this course are centered on the development of a strategic approach to planning, the utilization of knowledge acquired from previous business courses, and the application of theory to practical business situations.
