Transfer Credit Guidelines
Excelsior University is committed to providing students with the opportunity to maximize previously earned credit through the transfer process. We’ll review coursework from degree-granting institutions, military and job training records, and professional certifications and licenses to determine the applicability and number of credits awarded toward degree program requirements. Students must meet minimum program and institutional credit requirements for earning a degree, and Excelsior reserves the right to accept or deny credit from any specific institution or program.

How To Get Started
Step 1
Contact Your Partnership Representative
Your representative is here to answer all your partnership benefit questions and to help you get started at Excelsior. Reach out today using the contact form on this page.
Step 2
Review Program Admission Requirements
Review program admission requirements if you're interested in our nursing programs or graduate degrees , or to see if you're eligible for our partnership rates.

Do yourself a favor and begin filling out your application now. No need to wait—your information will be saved for you to finish later if you're interrupted.
