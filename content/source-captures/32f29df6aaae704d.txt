Objective:
The course objective is to build students' knowledge of key events and figures in the history of the United States from American prehistory through the era of Reconstruction. This course emphasizes critical thinking skills and consideration of multiple perspectives through the analysis of primary sources. Students learn how to think like a historian and apply a historian's lens to real-world situations.
Learning Outcomes:
Interpret the causes and consequences of the American Revolution and related primary sources within their historical context.
Examine how debates over government, citizenship, and political power shaped the early United States and why and how historical interpretations can differ.
Analyze primary and secondary sources related to slavery, expansion, and sectional conflict to understand how different groups experienced and explained the Civil War and Reconstruction.
Recognize the events, people, and motivations involved in the settling of the Americas and the tools and analytical lenses that historians use to research and interpret the past.
