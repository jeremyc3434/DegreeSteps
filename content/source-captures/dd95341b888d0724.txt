Program Description
The Bachelor of Science in Finance program provides students with a comprehensive understanding of financial theory, analysis, and practical application. The curriculum emphasizes critical thinking, problem-solving, financial decision-making, and ethical practice in a global business environment. Students also explore financial technologies, investment strategies, risk assessment, and market dynamics to prepare for a range of professional opportunities in finance.
Degree Program Objectives
In addition to the institutional and degree level learning objectives, graduates of this program are expected to achieve these learning outcomes:
Recognize the fundamentals of financial management in business environments.
Explore the financial environment and changing market conditions.
Execute investment decisions by assessing financial risk and leveraging.
Examine legal, ethical, and global issues in financial markets.

General Concentration (12 semester hours)
The General Finance Concentration will give students an overview of different financial practices including capital investments, risk, investments, and insurance. Those who select the general concentration will find themselves more positioned for a role in finance. This concentration will focus on how corporations utilize the financial markets for funding projects, capital improvements, and, in some cases, acquisitions.
Objectives
Upon successful completion of this concentration, the student will be able to:
Demonstrate a deeper knowledge around relevant topical areas in finance.

Accreditation
American Public University System (the System) is accredited by the Higher Learning Commission (HLC) ( www.hlcommission.org ), an institutional accreditation agency recognized by the U.S. Department of Education. HLC accredits degree-granting institutions nationwide and is also recognized by the Council for Higher Education Accreditation. The System is comprised of American Military University, American Public University, Rasmussen University, and Hondros College of Nursing. The System is authorized by the West Virginia Higher Education Policy Commission and other appropriate agencies in the states where it has campuses. The System is authorized to operate as a postsecondary educational institution by the Illinois Board of Higher Education. American Public University System, Inc., which operates the System, is a wholly owned subsidiary of American Public Education, Inc. American Public University System, American Public University, and American Military University are not affiliated with American University or the U.S. Military.
