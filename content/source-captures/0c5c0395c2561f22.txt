COURSE DESCRIPTION
Sophia Learning’s Lifespan Development course explores human development from the moment of conception
through infancy, childhood, adolescence, adulthood, and death. This course offers the opportunity for students
to dive into the process of physical, cognitive, psychological, and psychosocial development throughout life.
Students will be exposed to concepts about physical development, such as the impact of genetics, as well as
cultural differences in development and life experiences. This course provides students with the ability to
compare these concepts to their own personal lives while also allowing them to follow the process of life for
one individual through an eleven-part video series.
Content Notice
This course includes educational materials that depict human anatomy and development across the
lifespan. Some images and videos may include brief nudity (typically in the form of illustrations) for the
sole purpose of explaining biological, medical, and developmental concepts. These materials and
topics are presented strictly for academic and scientific learning and are not intended to be explicit or
sexual in nature. At Sophia Learning, we strive for mindfulness and sensitivity when designing your
learning experiences. However, if you have concerns about viewing such content, please consider this
notice before proceeding.
