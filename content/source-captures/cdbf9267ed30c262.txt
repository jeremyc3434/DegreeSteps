COURSE DESCRIPTION
Sophia Learning’s Introduction to Information Technology course provides an overview of information systems,
including hardware and software components, networking, and database management. You will explore how
computers represent, store, and process information, how organizations use technology to make decisions, and
how issues such as privacy, security, ethics, and artificial intelligence shape today’s IT landscape.
COURSE EFFECTIVE DATES: April 2026 - Present
PREREQUISITES: No prerequisites
LENGTH OF COURSE: This is a self-paced course. Students may use as much or as little time as needed to
complete the course.
ACE LEARNING EVALUATIONS RECOMMENDATION: In the lower-division baccalaureate category, 3 semester
hours in Introduction to Information Technology (ACE ID: SOPH-0023).
GRADING: This is a pass/fail course. Students must complete 11 Challenges (formative assessments), 5
Milestones (summative assessments), and 1 Touchstone (project-based or written assessments) with an overall
score of 70% or better.

Touchstones are projects that illustrate your comprehension of the course material, help you refine skills, and
demonstrate application of knowledge. Read further for information on the touchstones in this course:
Touchstone 4: Brightwell Studio IT Analysis (125 points): In this project, students will analyze the
technology needs of a small business, Brightwell Studio. They will evaluate the company’s current IT system
—including hardware, software, networking, and security—and propose practical solutions to improve
efficiency, communication, and data protection. This Touchstone allows students to apply core IT concepts
to a real-world scenario and demonstrate their understanding of how information systems support business
goals.

LEARNING OUTCOMES
Upon completion of the course, the student will be able to:
. Identify the basic components of an information system, including hardware, software, data, and networks.
. Explain how operating systems, databases, and applications work together to meet user and business
needs.
. Discuss how organizations use information systems to improve productivity and decision-making.
. Explore how emerging technologies like artificial intelligence are shaping modern IT and career paths.
