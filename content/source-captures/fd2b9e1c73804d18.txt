Program Description
The Bachelor of Arts in Marketing offers a comprehensive curriculum that seamlessly blends traditional marketing principles with cutting-edge digital strategies. Students will explore foundational areas such as branding, marketing strategy, and marketing research, alongside specialized courses in social media marketing, digital marketing, and integrated marketing communications. This program is designed to equip students with the skills necessary for success in dynamic industries, empowering them to drive growth through innovative marketing strategies, data-driven insights, and effective brand management.
This program has been designed in consultation with industry business leaders and is accredited by the Accreditation Council for Business Schools and Programs (ACBSP®) .
ACBSP® is a registered trademark of the Accreditation Council for Business Schools and Programs.
Degree Program Objectives
In addition to the institutional and degree level learning objectives, graduates of this program are expected to achieve these learning outcomes:
Integrate the marketing function with fundamental business enterprise concepts and principles of management, finance, strategic planning, and information systems.
Apply marketing principles to a particular business.
Use marketing research to identify and exploit business opportunities.
Critically analyze an organization’s marketing function and assemble and apply the various marketing strategies and techniques that align with the organization’s mission.
Develop a strategic marketing plan.

Students must choose a concentration for this degree program and may select from a General Concentration, Concentration in Digital Marketing Strategies, Concentration in Marketing Communication Strategy, or Concentration in Marketing Decision Making.
General Concentration (9 semester hours)
The General Concentration provides students with the opportunity to customize their educational journey by selecting courses that are in accordance with their specific career aspirations and interests. By completing 9 semester hours from a wide array of courses, students can attain a comprehensive understanding pertinent to their professional goals and personal interests.
Students must select 9 semester hours from undergraduate courses that have the following prefixes and have not already been used to fulfill their degree requirements: ACCT, ANLY, BUSN, DEFM, ECON, ENTR, FINC, HOSP, HRMT, MGMT, MKTG, RLMT, RTMG, SCMG and TLMT.
Objectives
Upon successful completion of this concentration, the student will be able to:
Demonstrate the ability to integrate knowledge from multiple disciplines to address complex challenges and make well-informed decisions in their professional environments.

Concentration in Digital Marketing Strategies (9 semester hours)
The Digital Marketing Strategies concentration equips students with the essential skills needed to leverage digital technologies for effective online marketing. This concentration covers key areas such as web analytics, search engine optimization (SEO), and the strategic use of digital media tools. Students will gain hands-on experience in analyzing web traffic, optimizing content for search engines, and utilizing digital platforms to engage audiences. By integrating these tools, students will be prepared to make data-driven decisions that enhance online visibility, improve user engagement, and drive the success of digital marketing campaigns.
Objectives
Upon successful completion of this concentration, the student will be able to:
Analyze digital marketing tools to optimize online campaigns and achieve measurable results in the digital landscape.

Concentration in Marketing Decision Making (9 semester hours)
The Marketing Decision Making concentration focuses on equipping students with the analytical skills and strategic thinking necessary to make informed marketing decisions. Students will learn to analyze data, evaluate market trends, and apply critical thinking techniques to address complex marketing challenges. By developing a strong understanding of business analysis, web analytics, and decision-making frameworks, students are prepared to make data-driven decisions that enhance marketing strategies and contribute to business success.

Upon successful completion of this concentration, the student will be able to:
Apply analytical tools and strategic frameworks to make informed marketing decisions.

Accreditation
American Public University System (the System) is accredited by the Higher Learning Commission (HLC) ( www.hlcommission.org ), an institutional accreditation agency recognized by the U.S. Department of Education. HLC accredits degree-granting institutions nationwide and is also recognized by the Council for Higher Education Accreditation. The System is comprised of American Military University, American Public University, Rasmussen University, and Hondros College of Nursing. The System is authorized by the West Virginia Higher Education Policy Commission and other appropriate agencies in the states where it has campuses. The System is authorized to operate as a postsecondary educational institution by the Illinois Board of Higher Education. American Public University System, Inc., which operates the System, is a wholly owned subsidiary of American Public Education, Inc. American Public University System, American Public University, and American Military University are not affiliated with American University or the U.S. Military.
