Objective:
The course objective is to immerse students in the world of visual storytelling, revealing how design shapes communication, user experiences, and brand identities. Students explore the core principles of UX/UI design, including user-centered design, touchpoints, and usability, while gaining practical insights into digital imaging techniques and the importance of accessibility in modern media. The course emphasizes the art of critique, guiding students through the process of analyzing visual elements, identifying strengths and weaknesses, and refining designs to align with project goals. Students uncover how visuals influence audience behavior, perception, and loyalty across platforms, developing the skills to create inclusive, compelling content that thrives in today’s dynamic digital landscape.
Learning Outcomes:
Analyze how visual communication strategies shape user perception, brand identity, and the effectiveness of messaging across different media formats.
Examine the integration of interactivity, accessibility, and multimedia elements to develop user-centered visual solutions for digital platforms.
Examine foundational design theories and models, including their psychological, cognitive, and historical influences on visual communication.
Identify key design elements and principles, including color, type, alignment, hierarchy, and balance, and their role in creating effective visual compositions.
