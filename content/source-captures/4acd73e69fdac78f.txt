Transfer Your College Credits
Wilmington University places value on your previous college-level coursework. You can transfer credits from several sources to apply to your undergraduate degree program or graduate degree program.
Our team will work with you to maximize your credit transfer and create your personalized degree completion pathway.

Apply your previous college or work experience toward your degree:
Transfer associate degree credits
Students can apply their entire associate degree (or all applicable credits) to a WilmU bachelor's degree.* Graduates of our partner schools enjoy additional transfer benefits.
Transfer some college credits
Use our Unofficial Transfer Credit Tool for an idea of how your prior courses may transfer to WilmU.
Transfer college credit earned in high school
WilmU accepts college-level credit earned through our Pre-College Programs and Delaware Pathways or a variety of standardized tests .
Receive WilmU credit for your experience
Your prior professional experience may be eligible for academic credit. See alternative paths to degree completion .
*Note: Certain limitations apply. The total number of transfer credits awarded is dependent on the Wilmington University program of study. Find additional information about admissions policies .
Why Transfer to WilmU?
Thousands of students choose to transfer credits and complete degrees at WilmU. In fact, about 70% of WilmU undergrads have transferred credits. Why?
70% of WilmU undergrad students transfer credits.
Guaranteed admission for all graduates of accredited community colleges.*
Generous transfer credit acceptance policies and seamless transfer processes.
Student success - 85% of students who transfer an associate degree to WilmU complete their bachelor’s degree.
Comprehensive Support: Student and alumni testimonials convey how WilmU supported their success
*Guaranteed admission is for students who have received an associate degree from a regionally accredited two-year school and meet WilmU's open-admissions requirements.
Recognitions
