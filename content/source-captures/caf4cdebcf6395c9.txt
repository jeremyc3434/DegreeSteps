American Council on Education (ACE)
Earn College Credit with UMPI’s ACE Program
The University of Maine at Presque Isle (UMPI) offers a streamlined process for students to gain college credit for prior learning through the American Council on Education (ACE) . This unique opportunity enables students to convert workforce training, exams, or courses completed through approved organizations—including businesses, unions, government bodies, and the military—into valuable college credits at UMPI. Start your education with a head start and reduce the time and cost of completing your degree.
How ACE Credit Works at UMPI
UMPI honors the American Council on Education’s recommendations for college credit, meaning you could transfer credits for various training programs, certifications, and military experiences . To begin, submit an official ACE transcript to the UMPI Registrar’s Office for Credit for Prior Learning Assessment (CPL) consideration.
Benefits of ACE Credit
Accelerate Your Degree : Apply ACE credits to reduce your total required courses, shortening your path to graduation.
Save on Tuition : Fewer courses mean a more affordable education, allowing you to maximize the value of your learning experiences.
Flexible & Recognized : UMPI’s ACE credit transfer process is recognized nationwide, supporting students from all backgrounds.
Steps to Apply for ACE Credit at UMPI
Prepare Your ACE Transcript : Gather official records of completed courses, workforce training, or military exams.
Submit to UMPI : Send your transcript to the UMPI Office of Student Records for evaluation.
Credit Evaluation : UMPI will review your submissions and apply credits where applicable.
An official ACE transcript must be submitted to the Office of Student Records for PLA consideration. For more information, contact Registrar Lisa Smith at (207) 768-9581 or email umpireg@maine.edu .
Other Credit Opportunities at UMPI
Explore additional credit options to further reduce your education cost and completion time:
Challenge Exams
Demonstrate knowledge in specific subjects to earn college credit.
CLEP and AP Credits
Transfer CLEP and AP credits to fulfill UMPI course requirements.
Military Credit
Receive credit for military training and DANTES exams.
