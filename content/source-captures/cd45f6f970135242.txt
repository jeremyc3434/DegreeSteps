Discover our 100% online  Degrees
The University of Maine at Presque Isle (UMPI) offers innovative and flexible degree programs in Liberal Studies designed to meet the unique needs of today’s students. Central to this flexibility is UMPI’s YourPace modality, an online, competency-based program that allows you to earn your degree at your own pace. Whether you’re a working professional, a transfer student, or someone looking to balance education with other commitments, UMPI’s YourPace program provides a personalized pathway to achieving your academic and career goals.
YourPace allows you to build on what you already know, completing coursework as quickly as your schedule and knowledge base allow. With no fixed deadlines or traditional semester structures, YourPace empowers you to progress as rapidly—or as steadily—as you wish. This model is ideal for adult learners, career changers, and anyone eager to take control of their education while receiving consistent support from UMPI’s dedicated faculty and advisors.
For those who prefer traditional on-campus learning, UMPI also offers Bachelor and Associate of Liberal Studies programs at its scenic northern Maine campus. Whether online or in-person, our Liberal Studies programs provide customizable options to fit your academic interests and career aspirations, making UMPI a top choice for students seeking a tailored and practical education.

What Is Competency-Based Education?
Designed to leverage your previous knowledge, college coursework, and work experience, competency-based education enables you to earn your degree at your pace as you demonstrate mastery of relevant academic content.
How our YourPace online programs save you time and money:
Prove your knowledge, move ahead—the faster you advance, the more you save
Flat tuition rate per 8-week session, no matter how many courses you complete
With no weekly deadlines, you control the speed of completion*
*Time to completion varies by student and is based on several factors including prior college coursework, relevant work experience, amount of time dedicated to studying, and the number of courses completed each session.

Accounting Minor, BLS
The Bachelor of Liberal Studies – Accounting Minor YourPace online program is for those who want a well-rounded education with a focus on numbers. Courses are 100% online and prepare you for roles involving budgeting, inventory, pensions, and taxation. You will work closely with your academic advisor and faculty to determine if your work history and previous college experience count toward your UMPI online degree.
Prepare for roles in accounting and increase your earning potential with this accredited YourPace online program. Qualify for positions that require an accounting background with the help of this flexible and affordable degree. You will learn theories of accounting, including analysis and preparation of financial statements, stockholder equity, and valuation of inventories. Topics also cover federal taxation, dispositions of property, and an examination of corporate, partnership, and estate taxation. Coursework is 100% online and includes the opportunity to establish and maintain an accounting system for a simulated business.
As a graduate of this online liberal studies program, you will be able to:
Analyze and prepare financial statements, statements of income and retained earnings, balance sheets, and statements of cash flow
Develop the skills to effectively communicate financial and non-financial results
Operate QuickBooks®
Apply the principles and tools of accounting and finance to real-world challenges
Accounting career opportunities include:
Bookkeeper

Manager in Accounting

The University of Maine at Presque Isle’s YourPace competency-based education program offers a Bachelor of Liberal Studies (BLS) in Criminal Justice, providing a flexible and affordable pathway for individuals ready to advance their careers in the criminal justice field. Through courses grounded in social justice and practical application, students develop a deeper understanding of criminal law, criminology, judicial systems, corrections, and the societal impacts of crime. The program also emphasizes critical thinking, communication, and analytical skills essential for success in both public and private sector roles.

Frequently Asked YourPace ALiberal Studies Questions
What is the YourPace Liberal Studies program?
The YourPace Liberal Studies program is a flexible, fully online, competency-based degree designed to fit your schedule and goals. It allows you to complete coursework at your own speed, leveraging prior knowledge and experience to accelerate your degree completion.
How does the YourPace program work?
YourPace is ideal for working professionals, adult learners, transfer students, and anyone seeking a flexible path to earning a degree. It’s particularly well-suited for individuals with prior college credits or significant work experience.
What is a Liberal Studies degree?
A Liberal Studies degree is an interdisciplinary program that allows you to explore a wide range of subjects, including humanities, social sciences, and natural sciences. It’s designed to provide critical thinking, communication, and problem-solving skills applicable to many careers.
How does the YourPace program enhance a Liberal Studies degree?
The YourPace program offers unparalleled flexibility, allowing you to tailor your Liberal Studies degree to your personal interests and career goals. You can progress at your own speed and customize your coursework to focus on areas that align with your future aspirations.

UMPI’s YourPace model allows students to progress through the program at their own speed, accommodating personal and professional commitments. With its broad foundation and flexible approach, this A.A. degree opens the door to endless possibilities in academics and beyond.
The topics you’ll study in our online MIS courses
UMPI’s YourPace Bachelor of Liberal Studies (B.L.S.) program with a Management Information Systems (MIS) Minor equips students with the knowledge and skills to manage technology-driven business processes. This minor focuses on IT management, data analysis, and systems integration, preparing students for roles in business technology, data management, and organizational efficiency.
The YourPace model allows students to progress at their own speed, integrating prior learning and professional experience into their academic journey. With a focus on the intersection of business and technology, the MIS Minor provides the tools to thrive in today’s digital economy.
To earn your BLS – Management Information Systems Minor, you need to complete 120 credit hours of coursework. Each course is built around a series of competencies. Once you have mastered the series, you complete the course. Using our personalized learning model, we look at your past college credit, prior learning, and work experience to create your custom degree plan.
Political Science, B.L.S. Minor
UMPI’s YourPace Bachelor of Liberal Studies (B.L.S.) program with a Political Science Minor offers a deep dive into the study of governance, public policy, and global politics. This minor hones analytical, communication, and decision-making skills, equipping students for careers in law, public service, advocacy, or education.
With the flexible YourPace model, students can progress at their own speed, tailoring the Political Science Minor to their personal and professional goals. Gain the knowledge and tools to make a meaningful impact in today’s dynamic political environment.
Minimum of 12 upper level credits (300-level and above)
Total: 18 Credit Hours
Pre-Law, B.L.S. Minor
UMPI’s YourPace Bachelor of Liberal Studies (B.L.S.) program offers a Pre-Law Minor that equips students with the foundational skills needed to excel in legal studies and related professions. Combining liberal arts education with focused legal studies, this minor emphasizes analytical thinking, persuasive communication, and ethical decision-making.
The Pre-Law Minor within the B.L.S. program prepares students for diverse career paths, including public service, advocacy, and consulting, while also serving as a stepping stone for graduate or law school. The flexible YourPace model allows students to integrate prior learning and professional experience into their academic journey, creating a customized path to success.
Whether aiming for law school or a career in fields requiring legal acumen, the Pre-Law Minor in the B.L.S. program provides the tools and confidence to make a lasting impact.
Project Management, B.L.S. Minor
UMPI’s YourPace Bachelor of Liberal Studies (B.L.S.) program with a Project Management Minor provides students with the essential skills to plan, execute, and manage projects across industries. This minor emphasizes leadership, organizational strategies, and problem-solving, preparing students for roles in business, nonprofit management, or operations.
With UMPI’s flexible YourPace model, students can progress through the Project Management Minor at their own speed, leveraging prior academic credits and professional experience. Gain the expertise to successfully lead projects and deliver results in today’s fast-paced professional environments.
To earn your BLS – Project Management Minor, you need to complete 120 credit hours of coursework. Each course is built around a series of competencies. Once you have mastered the series, you complete the course. Using our personalized learning model, we look at your past college credit, prior learning, and work experience to create your custom degree plan.
The program includes 40 credit hours of general education courses, 18 credit hours of Project Management Minor core courses, and 62 credit hours of electives.
Psychology, B.L.S. Minor
UMPI’s YourPace Bachelor of Liberal Studies (B.L.S.) program with a Psychology Minor offers students a deeper understanding of human behavior, thought processes, and emotional well-being. This minor emphasizes critical thinking, research skills, and the application of psychological principles, preparing students for roles in social services, education, or business.
The YourPace model allows students to progress through the Psychology Minor at their own speed, tailoring their learning experience to fit their goals. Whether you’re exploring career opportunities or preparing for advanced studies, this minor provides a flexible and enriching pathway to success.
Social Studies, B.L.S. Minor
UMPI’s YourPace Bachelor of Liberal Studies (B.L.S.) program with a Social Studies Minor provides students with a comprehensive understanding of history, geography, government, and sociology. This minor emphasizes critical thinking and analytical skills, preparing students for roles in education, public service, or community outreach.
