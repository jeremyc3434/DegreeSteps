Computer Science 204: Database Programming
3 lower level credits
Earn 3 transferable credits in our ACE-recommended database programming course (SDCM-0218).
Credits: 3 transferable, lower-division semester hours, recommended by ACE (Course ID: SDCM-0218)
Graded work: Three hands-on SQL assignments and open-book chapter tests, with no final exam
Passing grade: 70% or higher on your final course grade (assignments two-thirds, chapter tests one-third)
Prerequisites: There are no prerequisites for Database Programming
