Objective:
The course objective is to provide students with the fundamentals required to read, write, and speak in the Spanish language. This course offers students the opportunity to learn the basics of Spanish through both a linguistic and cultural lens, exploring the cultures and customs of several Spanish-speaking countries along with basic grammatical structures and useful vocabulary for making introductions, describing university and home life, communicating current and future plans, and discussing various activities and interests.
Learning Outcomes:
Express likes and dislikes, share activities and pastimes, state current actions, and communicate future plans.
Recognize major cultural aspects of the Spanish-speaking world, including customs and traditions, significant locations, and other facets of daily life.
Greet others, make introductions, say where someone is from, describe physical and personality traits, and state habitual actions.
Specify times with habitual actions, use possessive adjectives to communicate relationships, describe family members, and describe one's home.
General Topics:
The Spanish Alphabet
Accents, Emphasis, and Other Pronunciation Rules
