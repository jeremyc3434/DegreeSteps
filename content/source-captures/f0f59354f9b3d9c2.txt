Objective:
Psychology 316: Advanced Social Psychology (SDCM-0182) explores how interpersonal communication is influenced by social contexts and applies social psychology to real-world issues in relationships, work, education, and online interactions.
Learning Outcomes:
Apply theories of the social self to everyday situations.
Analyze how stereotyping, prejudice, and discrimination impact human thoughts, feelings, and behaviors in legal, career, health, education, and online contexts.
Explain the factors involved in forming and maintaining close interpersonal relationships (e.g., friendships, romantic relationships, marriage), integrating cultural perspectives to assess their impact on social dynamics and individual behavior.
Compare the psychological processes driving prosocial and aggressive behavior as well as the effects of these behaviors across cultural, educational, and occupational settings.
Apply social psychological research methods to everyday situations and current events in social environments.
Describe how social cognition and social perception shape thinking and behavior across diverse social contexts.
Evaluate the impact of social influences like persuasion, group dynamics, and intergroup relations on cognition, emotions, and behaviors.
Evaluate empirical findings on how conformity, obedience, and aggression influence social behavior and relate to broader societal issues.
