Build Your Foundation in Accounting
Start your journey in accounting with Champlain College Online’s Associate of Science in Accounting. This program, built around the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) Core Competency Framework, offers a blend of hands-on learning, problem-solving, and critical thinking to prepare you for success in the field.
Finish your program faster and for less money with credit for prior learning and experience .
Differentiate your degree and get your resume noticed with a certificate. Speak with your advisor for information.
Consider a career using the skills you learn in this degree
Essential Skills for a Career in Accounting
Foundational Accounting Knowledge: Develop essential accounting skills in bookkeeping, financial analysis, and payroll services.
Real-World Problem Solving: Apply analytical and critical thinking to assess risks and ensure sound financial reporting and internal controls.
Pathway to CPA and Career Growth: Build a strong foundation to advance toward CPA licensure or continue with a bachelor's degree in accounting.
Integrated Communication Skills: Learn to convey complex financial information clearly to stakeholders, enhancing your impact in professional environments.
Business Acumen and Ethical Standards: Understand ethical principles, risk management, and the broader business context surrounding accounting.

Learning Outcomes
Graduates of the accounting associate online program will demonstrate the following industry-specific skills, knowledge, and competencies.
The Foundation Builder: Apply core accounting principles and practices, including financial and managerial accounting, to record, classify, and report financial information accurately.
The Analytical Thinker: Use quantitative and analytical tools to interpret financial data, assess basic organizational performance, and support informed business decisions.
The Ethical Practitioner: Apply ethical standards and an understanding of legal and regulatory frameworks to accounting tasks, ensuring the integrity of financial information.
Program competencies are adapted from the American Institute of Certified Public Accountants (AICPA) Core Competency Framework and include:
Functional competencies: technical competencies most aligned with the value contributed by accounting professionals.
Personal competencies: individual attributes and values
Broad business perspective competencies: perspectives and skills relating to understanding of internal and external contexts.
Considering Becoming a CPA?
CPA Exam and Licensure Requirements
A CPA is a Certified Public Accountant, a designation licensed by each state. Champlain College students who complete the associate degree in accounting program may decide to continue on to earn their CPA. Following are the requirements both the exam and licensure:
CPA Exam: Champlain College’s 120 credit online bachelor's in accounting program meets the credit requirement to sit for the CPA exam in Vermont. To determine if our program meets your specific state’s credit requirements, visit the NASBA website to learn more about what is required to sit for the CPA exam in your state.
CPA Licensure: There are several requirements to become a licensed CPA in the state of Vermont including educational credits and professional accounting experience. Champlain College’s Bachelor of Science in Accounting, and Master’s of Science in Accounting meet the educational requirements for Vermont. Vermont currently has three education pathways, all of which require passing the Uniform CPA Examination:
One hundred and fifty (150) college credits at a college or university recognized by the Board, including a baccalaureate degree with a minimum of forty-two (42) semester hours of accounting, auditing, and related subjects as the Board determines to be appropriate;
A baccalaureate degree from a college or university recognized by the Board with a concentration in accounting or an equivalent meeting the requirements prescribed by the Board.
A post-baccalaureate degree from a college or university recognized by the Board with a concentration in accounting or an equivalent meeting the requirements prescribed by the Board
In addition to the educational requirements outlined above, professional accounting experience is also required to become licensed in the state of Vermont. In order to determine experience requirements, contact the Vermont licensure board . For licensure requirements in other states, please check the NASBA website .

Accelerate Your Degree
Time to Completion
A 60-credit associate degree in accounting traditionally takes 2 years to complete. Your time to completion is dependent on how many courses you take per term and whether you have credits to transfer.
CCO accepts up to 45 transfer credits toward an associate degree. If you transfer the maximum, you could have as few as 5 courses (about 3 terms) left to finish. The average CCO associate degree student brings in 19 credits. That leaves about 41 credits, or 14 courses, to complete. If you take two classes per 7-week term , that works out to just over 1 year on average to earn an associate degree . Your own timeline may be shorter or longer, depending on your situation.
We try to make online learning as seamless and quick as you want it to be. Because we believe, no matter where you've started, CCO is where you can finish strong – and save time and tuition along the way.

Frequently Asked Questions
Following are some frequently asked questions for our associate of science in accounting.
Can I become a CPA with an associate degree in accounting?
While an online associate’s degree in accounting provides foundational knowledge, it typically doesn’t offer enough credit hours to meet the requirements for state CPA licensure. However, an associate degree can open up more job opportunities than being a bookkeeper. Graduates often choose to further their education by pursuing a bachelor’s degree or other certificates .
What job opportunities are available for those with an associate degree in accounting?
Champlain’s online associate degree in accounting prepares you for various entry-level roles, including:
Accounting Assistant
Payroll Assistant
Tax Preparer
Assistant Cost Analyst
Data Entry Clerk
Additionally, some graduates may choose to transfer to our bachelor’s degree program for further specialization.
Is an online associate degree in accounting flexible?
Yes, Champlain’s online associate accounting degree tends to offer more flexibility than a traditional on-campus program. Distance programs often appeal to students who work full-time, have busy schedules, or are unable to commute to campus. Online programs can be self-paced, but our virtual classrooms have set assignment deadlines to mirror the structure of in-person classes.
Can I complete a certificate while I’m pursuing the online associate degree in accounting?
Yes! Champlain College’s online associate degree in accounting equips you with practical skills and knowledge, preparing you for a successful career in the accounting field. For those interested in enhancing their accounting skills, Champlain also offers four unique undergraduate online accounting certificate programs which provide a deeper dive into specific accounting topics for entry-level positions and career advancement.
How many course can I take each term?
You may take 1 course, or you may take 2-3 courses per 7-week term. Students may not take more than three courses (9 credit hours) in a term, or 6 courses (18 credit hours) in a 15-week semester without prior program director approval. Students should always consult their Student Success Advisor with any questions.
