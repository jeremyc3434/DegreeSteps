Mission
The Bachelor of Applied Science in Cybersecurity program prepares
students for career opportunities, advancement and professional growth
in the cybersecurity sector to help secure and protect organizations from
a wide-variety of threats and attacks.
Program Learning Outcomes
• Confidentiality: Employ security strategies to protect the
confidentiality of network devices and data.
• Integrity: Utilize industry best practices to secure data and ensure it
is accurate and trustworthy.
• Availability: Maintain the accessibility of organization data,
information, and systems.
• Risk: Evaluate risk of a given threat or vulnerability to an
organization's resources.
• Organizational Security: Develop security policies and procedures to
promote cybersecurity throughout the organization.
• Human Security: Protect IT resources from human flaws and risks.
Degree Requirements
In addition to the University General Education (42 credits) and free
elective (30 credits) requirements, the degree consists of 39 credits of
coursework in the core program of study and 9 program elective credits
(120 total credits).
Eligible industry certification exams may earn college credit that can be
applied to this program. Click here (https://catalog.umassglobal.edu/
business-professional-studies/#missionstatementtext) for more
information.
