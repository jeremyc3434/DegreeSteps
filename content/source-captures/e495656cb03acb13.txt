Objective:
The course objective is to explore human development from the moment of conception through infancy, childhood, adolescence, adulthood, and death. This course offers the opportunity for students to dive into the process of physical, cognitive, psychological, and psychosocial development throughout life. Students are exposed to concepts about physical development, such as the impact of genetics, as well as cultural differences in development and life experiences. This course provides students with the ability to compare these concepts to their own personal lives while also allowing them to follow the process of life for one individual through an eleven-part video series.
Learning Outcomes:
Identify relevant human development theories and the influence of genetics on human conception.
Identify maternal health factors and the stages of development in infancy.
Analyze the stages of development in childhood and adolescence.
Examine the stages of development and social interactions in early and middle adulthood.
Identify the stages of development in late adulthood and the stages of death.
