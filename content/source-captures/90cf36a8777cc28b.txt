OVERVIEW
Design, Build, Secure Tomorrow's Networks with AI and Automation
Looking to launch—or level up—a career in tech? The B.S. in Cloud and Network Engineering (BSCNE) from WGU helps you build the skills employers need most. This degree prepares you for today’s hybrid infrastructure environments, where cloud systems, on-premise networks, and AI-driven automation work together to power the modern workplace. You’ll graduate with hands-on experience, industry certifications, and the technical skills to thrive in high-demand cloud and network roles.
You’ll develop real-world expertise in:
Network design and security
Cloud architecture and deployment
Automation and AI for IT operations

Four Concentrations to Choose From
General Track : Build broad foundational skills across cloud and traditional networking. Ideal for flexible IT roles that require a wide skill set.
AWS Track : Focus on Amazon Web Services technologies. Perfect for students aiming for roles in AWS cloud architecture, automation, and security.
Azure Track: Specialize in Microsoft Azure environments. Designed for those pursuing careers in Azure cloud management and infrastructure.
Cisco Track : Dive deep into Cisco-based networking systems. Best for students targeting careers in advanced networking, infrastructure, and systems support.

Your courses will depend on the focus area you choose, but all are designed to teach the skills that employers are hiring for right now. From core IT foundations to advanced topics in cloud computing and network security, every course is built to help you succeed on the job.
Our Bachelor of Science in Cloud and Network Engineering degree program was designed with input from our Information Technology Program Council and other networking expert practitioners. We‘ve also studied the job market, and the program content aligns with the network engineering and security job market and skills requirements. The program covers crucial subjects, including automation, IT fundamentals, networks and security, spreadsheets, scripting and programming, data management, operating systems, interconnecting network devices, leadership and management, web development, the business of IT, and security. This cloud and network engineering program is built to give you the technical skills to succeed in your career.

Earning a bachelor’s degree designed by industry experts adds theoretical depth to the practical knowledge you may already have. The experts on our IT Program Council know what it takes for a graduate to be successful in the fast-paced, ever-changing world of network engineering and security.
The B.S. Cloud and Network Engineering program is an all-online program offering hands-on experience in a virtual environment. You’ll complete program requirements independently, with instruction and support from WGU faculty, and be expected to maintain on-time progress. Each course is typically three or four units. There’s no limit on the number of units you can complete each term, so the more courses you complete in a term, the fewer terms it takes you to graduate.
