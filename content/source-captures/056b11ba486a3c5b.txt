COURSE DESCRIPTION
Sophia Learning’s Precalculus course prepares you for the skills needed for Calculus I. In this course, you will
build off the topics in Sophia’s College Algebra by evaluating and graphing functions, and solving systems of
equations using matrices. You will then investigate different types of functions by graphing power, polynomial,
and rational functions. By solving exponential and logarithmic equations, you will also model real-world
situations. Then, you will move into the trigonometry portion of Precalculus by analyzing trigonometric functions
through graphs, equations, and identities and solving oblique triangles using the law of sines and the law of
cosines. You will also apply trigonometric concepts to the polar coordinates system and the trigonometric form
of complex numbers. Finally, you will wrap up the course with supplementary topics which include vectors,
writing equations of conic sections, working with arithmetic and geometric sequences and series, and
expanding powers of binomials.

LEARNING OUTCOMES
Upon completion of the course, the student will be able to:
. Evaluate and graph functions and solve systems of equations.
. Graph power, polynomial, and rational functions and model real-world situations.
. Find derivatives of functions and apply the differentiation process when appropriate.
. Analyze trigonometric functions through graphs, equations, and identities.
. Write equations of conic sections, write the nth term and find the sum of arithmetic and geometric
sequences, and expand powers of binomials.
