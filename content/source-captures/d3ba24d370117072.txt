When you have completed these steps, you are admitted in the School of Christian Development which encompasses all the free full and mini courses, awards, certificates, and diplomas. Interested in college degree admissions?
Follow the steps outlined above! Once you have created your account, enroll in the Leadership Excellence School Admissions & Degree Orientation.
View the Admissions & Orientation Course
This course will walk you through the steps for degree admissions, including:
Which degree program is right for you
Fee payment
Verifying your identity
Updating your student profile
Establishing your college readiness
Uploading your letter of recommendation
Once this course is completed, you will be admitted to the college program (Leadership Excellence School).
