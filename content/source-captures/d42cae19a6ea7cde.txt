Transfer credit process
There are three steps to finding out if your previous university credits will apply to your Capella program:
1. Obtain official transcripts
Capella simplifies this step. When you apply for admission to Capella University, you can give Capella permission to request your transcripts from other schools on your behalf.*
*Capella will not be able to obtain your transcript if there is a hold on your record from another university.
2. Credit evaluation
Capella evaluates your previous coursework based on applicability to degree requirements, grades earned and how closely a course matches our courses per department, title, level, credits and description of course content. Courses without a direct Capella match may be evaluated as electives in programs that include electives.*
* Degree levels and programs have maximum transfer credit limits. See Transfer Credit and Credit for Prior Learning Policy for details.
3. Credit evaluation summary
You’ll receive an email with a summary of the courses that have been accepted for transfer each time we receive a new official transcript.

Frequently asked questions
When do I send my transcripts?
Once you have completed the Capella admissions application – including your education history – you can start sending your transcripts to us. Capella evaluates each transcript for transfer credit as it arrives.
Does Capella request my transcripts for me?
Yes. If your previous institution allows third-party requests, we will be able to request them on your behalf. You can choose this option as part of your application to Capella.
Will Capella accept credits from any institution?
Generally, we can accept transfer credits from other regionally accredited institutions and from other programs on a case-by-case basis.
How many credits can I transfer into my program?
Each of our programs has a different limit to how many transfer credits can be applied.
A bachelor’s student can transfer in up to 135 quarter credits out of the 180 required to complete the program. This may be limited by program and specific courses.
Typically, about a quarter of a master’s degree can be fulfilled via credit transfer.
Doctoral programs vary. Some programs accept transfer credits, and some do not. Connect with us for details about transferring credits into your program.

Transfer credit policy
How long does Capella take to evaluate my courses?
Once we have received your transcripts, you can expect to see evaluations within seven business days.
How do I get my Capella transcript from you?
You can order your official transcript from www.parchment.com , our secure transcript request service. If you don’t already have an account with this service, you’ll be able to set up a new one for free. Please note that former Capella students do not need to request Capella transcripts when applying to a subsequent program or degree at Capella.
What nationally recognized programs do you accept as credit toward a degree?
The following is a list of our recognized programs for credit toward a Capella bachelor’s degree program. Minimum scores may be required.
Recognized programs:
Advanced Placement examinations
International Baccalaureate examinations
College-Level Examination Program
DANTES Subject Standardized Tests
ACE-recommended learning experiences including those earned through the military, Sophia for College Credit, StraighterLine and many more
What credits are eligible for transfer?
Eligible transfer credit:
Credit awarded from an academic institution or program that was accredited prior to the applicant’s withdrawal from the institution
Undergraduate coursework completed with a grade of "C–" or better
Graduate coursework completed with a grade of "B–" or better
Not eligible for transfer credit:
Credit awarded by another institution’s petition for credit, portfolio assessments or institution-specific credit by examination
Graduate-level courses applied toward a previous undergraduate degree
Work with your enrollment specialist if you have questions about your transfer credit evaluation, or if you wish to explore options for any non-accepted credits.
What are other types of credits you’ll consider?
Credit that may be considered:
Credit awarded by an institution or program with provisional accreditation at the time the applicant withdrew from the institution
Credit awarded by other institutions and credit by a standardized exam such as CLEP or DSST
Vocational courses from technical colleges and programs
Courses completed outside of the U.S.
If I’ve already completed my bachelor’s degree, does Capella offer accelerated degree programs?
Yes, Capella offers an accelerated BSN (ABSN) pathway for students pursuing the BSN (Prelicensure) program who already have their bachelor’s degree. Talk to an enrollment specialist to find out if you’re eligible.
