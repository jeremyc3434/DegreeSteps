Program Description
The Bachelor of Arts in Human Resource Management is designed for human resource professionals interested in understanding all of the functions within the field. The program will provide an overview of issues impacting the global workforce from a generalist and specialist perspective. While in the program, students will explore strategies to motivate, compensate, recruit, appraise, and develop an organization's talent base. Collaboration and case studies will support the importance of the human resource function to the strategic direction and effectiveness of an organization.
This program has been designed in consultation with industry business leaders and is accredited by the Accreditation Council for Business Schools and Programs (ACBSP®) .
ACBSP® is a registered trademark of the Accreditation Council for Business Schools and Programs.
Degree Program Objectives
In addition to the institutional and degree level learning objectives, graduates of this program are expected to achieve these learning outcomes:
Apply human resource management principles in recruitment, selection, training, and development, ensuring ethical standards.
Assess employment laws and regulations, including labor standards and workplace safety, to ensure compliance.
Utilize human resources technology, data analytics, and HR systems to enhance HR functions and support ethical decision making.
Create strategic human resource management plans that align with organizational goals, incorporating ethical considerations and global HR principles, including cultural awareness and international labor standards.
Design fair and competitive compensation and benefits programs that comply with legal standards.

Students must choose a concentration for this degree program and may select from a General Concentration, Concentration in Employee Relations and Change Leadership, Concentration in Leadership and Development, or Concentration in Workplace Conflict Management.
General Concentration (9 semester hours)
The General Concentration provides students with the opportunity to customize their educational journey by selecting courses that are in accordance with their specific career aspirations and interests. By completing 9 semester hours from a wide array of courses, students can attain a comprehensive understanding pertinent to their professional goals and personal interests.
Students must select 9 semester hours from undergraduate courses that have the following prefixes and have not already been used to fulfill their degree requirements: ACCT, ANLY, BUSN, DEFM, ECON, ENTR, FINC, HOSP, HRMT, MGMT, MKTG, RLMT, RTMG, SCMG and TLMT.
Objectives
Upon successful completion of this concentration, the student will be able to:
Demonstrate the ability to integrate knowledge from multiple disciplines to address complex challenges and make well-informed decisions in their professional environments.

Concentration in Employee Relations and Change Leadership (9 semester hours)
The Employee Relations and Change Leadership concentration is designed to equip students with the knowledge and skills necessary to foster a positive and productive workplace environment. This concentration emphasizes the importance of effective communication, leadership, and organizational change management, while also addressing the complexities of employment and labor relations.
Objectives
Upon successful completion of this concentration, the student will be able to:
Apply effective communication, leadership, and change management strategies to create and maintain a positive and productive workplace environment, while navigating the complexities of employment and labor relations.

Concentration in Leadership and Development (9 semester hours)
The Leadership and Development concentration equips students with the ability to build leadership skills and foster leadership development programs within organizations. Students will learn to apply concepts in organizational development, motivation, and human resource management to cultivate talent and improve performance. Emphasis is placed on professional development, coaching, and succession planning as tools for developing leaders who can positively impact organizational growth. Graduates will be prepared for roles in leadership and development, performance management, and human resources.
Objectives
Upon successful completion of this concentration, the student will be able to:
Demonstrate the skills and knowledge to effectively lead, motivate, and develop human capital, driving organizational success and fostering a positive work environment.

Concentration in Workplace Conflict Management (9 semester hours)
The Workplace Conflict Management concentration prepares students to address conflict resolution and dispute resolution in organizational settings. Students gain essential skills in managing interpersonal conflict, negotiation, and mediation, while applying human resource management principles. The curriculum explores the impact of employment and labor laws, organizational culture, and communication strategies on conflict dynamics. By emphasizing conflict management skills, students will be prepared for career opportunities in HR departments, government agencies, or other organizations where effective workplace conflict management drives positive change.
Objectives
Upon successful completion of this concentration, the student will be able to:
Resolve workplace conflicts using advanced techniques
Understand employment and labor laws to create a collaborative and productive work environment
Demonstrate strong interpersonal and communication skills to manage diverse teams and drive positive organizational change.

Accreditation

HLC accredits degree-granting institutions nationwide and is also recognized by the Council for Higher Education Accreditation. The System is comprised of American Military University, American Public University, Rasmussen University, and Hondros College of Nursing. The System is authorized by the West Virginia Higher Education Policy Commission and other appropriate agencies in the states where it has campuses. The System is authorized to operate as a postsecondary educational institution by the Illinois Board of Higher Education.
