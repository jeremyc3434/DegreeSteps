Bring Your Credits. Finish Faster.
Some universities only accept a handful of transfer credits. At Colorado State University Global, you can bring up to 90 credits with you, including prior college coursework, military education, professional training, and real-world experience.
That generosity matters. It means you can save money, finish faster, and move closer to the promotion, career change, or salary increase you’re working toward without having to start over. With maximum transfer credit, you’re paying only for the final stretch of your degree, not the work you’ve already done.
CSU Global meets you where you are—so you can get where you want to go.
Transferring from a Colorado community college?
CSU Global partners with the Colorado Community College System (CCCS) to provide seamless transfer pathways for students moving from an associate degree to an online bachelor’s program. Key benefits include:
Guaranteed admission for CCCS associate degree graduates
10% tuition discount for CCCS alumni with a 2.0+ GPA
Participation in the Statewide Transfer Articulations program (Business & Criminal Justice AA)
Credit transfer support to maximize previous coursework

FAQ
How does the transfer credit process work at CSU Global, and what should students know before starting?
When a student applies to attend CSU Global, the official transcripts provided are reviewed for possible transfer credit; a line-by-line evaluation of each transcript is completed. Each individual course is reviewed for possible transfer to CSU Global as a General Education course, major course, or elective course. The Transfer Evaluation team uses a national database to review course descriptions from the institution attended to determine the transferability of a course. When a course description is not available, a syllabus will be requested that the student can provide for further review.
What’s unique about CSU Global’s transfer credit policies compared to other universities?
CSU Global has a very generous transfer credit policy; students can transfer up to 90 credits toward their bachelor’s program and up to 50% of their master’s program. For bachelor students, there are many alternative credit options for earning General Education and elective credits, making completing a bachelor’s degree more cost-effective and decreasing the time needed to earn a bachelor’s degree.
For a program with a 30-credit major, a student could transfer in all 31 General Education requirements and 59 elective credits and only need to complete 30 major credits at CSU Global. Every program has different major requirements; the required electives will vary depending on the program. Elective requirements can range from 59 to 26; the fewer electives needed means more credits are required in the major.
How does CSU Global convert quarter system credits to semester credits, and what should students know about how this affects their degree progress?
If a student attended a quarter-credit school, CSU Global converts those to semester credits. Typically, a quarter is 10 weeks versus a 15-week semester; therefore, a 3-quarter credit class is a 2-semester hour class due to the amount of time a student spends in that course. CSU Global utilizes a formula to convert quarter credits to semester credits, which the majority of schools also use, so there is nothing specific to CSU Global.
Can a student get a transfer credit evaluation or an idea ahead of time how many credits will transfer before they’re formally admitted?
Yes, a student can work with an Enrollment Counselor to have a Transfer Estimate completed prior to enrolling at CSU Global. This is an estimate of how many credits will transfer; however, the student would need an Official Evaluation of their transcripts to know what specific course requirements would be needed.
Are there any specific types of courses or programs that don’t transfer, and how can students check before applying?
The transferability of a course depends on the accreditation an institution holds, and a CSU Global Enrollment Counselor can assist in checking the accreditation. Courses that do not transfer are any bachelor’s course with a grade of D+ or lower and any graduate course with a grade of C+ or lower. For bachelor students, developmental courses do not transfer as well as courses with an S, P, or CR grade in some circumstances. Every institution has a different course numbering system; therefore, what is determined to be developmental varies based on the institution attended.
How can I see for myself which of my previous courses may transfer to CSU Global?
Want to get a clearer picture of how your previous coursework may transfer to CSU Global? The CollegeSource Transfer Evaluation System lets you search evaluated courses from other institutions and see how they may apply to your degree. It’s a helpful way to explore transfer possibilities before you apply. For courses not listed, our team can review them individually during the evaluation process.
Please note: Results are not guaranteed to include all courses offered. If you have completed a course in the past three years that is not included on the course equivalency list, please submit a review request to transcript.evaluation@csuglobal.edu, and include the course name, description, and syllabus. Transfer equivalencies are subject to change at any time without warning.

Alternative Credit Options
We believe that learning can happen through a wide variety of mediums – that’s why CSU Global accepts various forms of alternative credit options that enable students to earn their degrees quickly and at a reduced cost.
Alternative Credit Options
Credit for Military Experience
CSU Global may accept college credits for military service that have been evaluated according to American Council on Education Guidelines. You’ve helped secure our future, let us help you secure yours!
Military College Credit Transfer
International Students
We’ve partnered with the International Education Research Foundation to streamline and complete international transcript evaluations, which also helps reduce costs for students.
International Students
Reverse Transfer Students
Reverse Transfer allows students who transferred from a Colorado two-year institution to a four-year institution to combine credits, meaning you can earn the associate degree you started while working toward your bachelor’s degree.
Colorado Reverse Transfer Students
