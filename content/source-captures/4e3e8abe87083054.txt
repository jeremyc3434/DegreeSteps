Sophia Learning Transfer Guide
Credit Evaluation Guide of ACE Credit Recommendations for Sophia Learning
The University of Arizona Global Campus (UAGC) recognizes the uniqueness of each student’s experience and realizes
learning takes place in a variety of environments. The American Council on Education (ACE) provides a means for
recommending credit for exams, courses, trainings, and/or experience acquired outside of a traditional university
classroom setting.
Students may pursue their academic and professional goals through a variety of degree programs at UAGC. For
additional information on UAGC degree programs, please visit our website.
Students are responsible for requesting their Sophia Learning transcripts.
Please request your Sophia Learning transcripts be sent to UAGC through your Sophia account.
Transfer Provisions
The recommendations listed below are based upon content reviewed on the date of transfer guide creation. Any
variation in previous or subsequent versions may impact the applicability of incoming credit. Credit is not guaranteed
until official transcripts are reviewed and credit is applied to a student’s official degree requirements. Official transfer
credit evaluation takes place once a student is enrolled at UAGC, and all official transcripts have been received.
For full information on specific UAGC program requirements and transfer credit provisions, please visit the UAGC
Academic Catalog and the Undergraduate General Transfer Credit Provisions and Limitations in the UAGC Academic
Catalog.
Additional Information
• Before completing additional Sophia Learning courses, students should confirm any ACE recommendation is still
active and work with their Academic Advisor to confirm applicability to students’ unique remaining degree
requirements.
• Credit will only be considered if the student successfully passes the incoming option(s) (exam, course, training,
and/or experience) with a passing score.
• Semester Credits listed is the maximum number of credits students may receive at UAGC from the incoming
option(s).
• Incoming options that meet more than one area of recommendation can only be applied to one area, unless the
option carries sufficient credit for additional area(s).
• Multiple incoming options may meet the same area of recommendation. Only one option with the required
credit is needed to satisfy each area.
• For recommendations marked with an asterisk (*), additional information must be confirmed before credit may
be applied.
• Certain UAGC coursework may require incoming transfer credit to be completed within a specific time frame.
• In some instances, lower-division credits marked with a cross (†) may be applied for upper-division UAGC
coursework. Lower-division credits will not apply to the upper-division credit requirement. Additional upper-
division credits may be required.
• Exams, courses, trainings, and/or experience not listed and which carry college-level recommended credit will
be evaluated for applicability to the unique requirements of each student’s chosen degree (e.g., elective,
general education, or major).
• Vocational-level recommendations are not transferable for credit at UAGC.
• Credit may only be applied to the same degree level (undergraduate, graduate) as the incoming transfer credit.
