OPM-4150 Logistics

This course focuses on the corporate functions of demand and supply management, inventory control, warehousing, and transportation and, in particular, how these functions are changing to accommodate the integration and coordination of activities in a global supply chain.
Advisory: It is advisable to have knowledge in a course equivalent to OPM-3010: Operations Management and CIS-3010: Management Information Systems with a grade of C or better to succeed in this course. Students are responsible for ensuring they have the required knowledge.
