Additional Admission Requirements for the MBA-CB
All applicants are expected to meet the following admission
requirements:
• Must have completed one of the following:
• A bachelor's degree, or higher, in business or business
management from a college or university with approved
institutional accreditation, or that is a candidate for
approved institutional accreditation, or comparable
bachelor's degree earned at a recognized foreign institution.
If the institution became accredited while the applicant was
attending, a minimum of 75% of the coursework taken
toward the degree must have been completed while the
institution was accredited or during the candidacy period. In
addition, a minimum equivalent of three years of full-time
professional administrative, operational, leadership, or
management experience work experience within the past ten
years in a business-related environment.
• A bachelor's degree, or higher, in any discipline, from a
college or university with approved institutional
accreditation, or candidate for accreditation, or comparable
bachelor's degree earned at a recognized foreign institution
or a master's degree from an ABA accredited institution (i.e.,
JD, LLB, LLM). If the institution became accredited while
the applicant was attending, a minimum of 75% of the
coursework taken toward the degree must have been
completed while the institution was accredited or during the
candidacy period. In addition, a minimum equivalent of five
years of full-time professional administrative, operational,
leadership, or management experience work experience
within the past ten years in a business-related environment.
• A cumulative GPA of 2.5 for all coursework listed on the degree
posted transcript at the same level as the conferred degree (i.e.,
bachelor's, master's, or doctoral) is required for admission.
Transfer coursework that was earned outside of the institution
that conferred the degree will not be calculated into the
cumulative GPA for admissibility.

Minimum Grade Requirements for the MBA-CB
A grade of Met (ME) or better is required for all competencies in
every course.
Students will be placed on Scholastic Probation (SP) program aca-
demic status if a grade of NM is earned in a course. If a student
repeats a course due to receiving a NM grade and receives a grade
of NM on the second attempt, the student will be administratively
withdrawn from the program and placed in Scholastic Suspension.
Scholastic Suspension student academic status will be effective as
of the latest course end date for any of the courses that students are
concurrently enrolled in at the time the NM grade posts in the
repeated course.
Students who have been placed on SP status must retake the course
that placed them on Scholastic Probation at the earliest available
scheduling opportunity.
Residency Requirements and Assessment Attempts for the
MBA-CB
• All required domains, courses, and competencies must be
completed at UOPX to be eligible for degree conferral.
• Students must complete all required UOPX competency-based
courses in the required course of study to complete the program.
Students cannot waive any courses in the required course of
study using transfer activities, or any UOPX coursework from
outside of this competency-based program.
• Students will have a maximum of three attempts to pass the final
assessment for each competency. The first two attempts may be
taken by the student after a discussion with the faculty mentor.
• Prior to a third attempt at passing the assessment, students must
complete a remediation plan and receive formal approval from
the faculty mentor.
Re-entry for the MBA-CB
• Any student in a Competency-Based program is considered to be
a re-entry student if no academically related activity has been
posted for more than 365 days from their last recorded
academically related activity.
Competency-Based program students who meet this criteria for
being considered a re-entry student are subject to all of the
requirements for re-entry students as outlined in the
University's standard Academic Program Re-Entry policy.
