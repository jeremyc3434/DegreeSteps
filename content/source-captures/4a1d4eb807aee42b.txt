COURSE DESCRIPTION
Sophia Learning's U.S. History I course builds your knowledge of key events and figures in the history of the
United States from American prehistory through the era of Reconstruction. This course emphasizes critical
thinking skills and consideration of multiple perspectives through the analysis of primary sources. You will learn
how to think like a historian and apply a historian's lens to real-world situations.
COURSE EFFECTIVE DATES: July 2026 - Present
PREREQUISITES: No prerequisites
LENGTH OF COURSE: This is a self-paced course. Students may use as much or as little time as needed to
complete the course.
ACE CREDIT® RECOMMENDATION: In the lower-division baccalaureate degree category, 3 semester hours in
U.S. History Before 1877 (ACE ID: SOPH-0022).
GRADING: This is a pass/fail course. Students must complete 12 Challenges (formative assessments), 5
Milestones (summative assessments), and 2 Touchstones (project-based or written assessment) with an overall
score of 70% or better.

LEARNING OUTCOMES
Upon completion of the course, the student will be able to:
. Recognize the events, people, and motivations involved in the settling of the Americas and the tools and
analytical lenses that historians use to research and interpret the past.
. Interpret the causes and consequences of the American Revolution and related primary sources within their
historical context.
. Examine how debates over government, citizenship, and political power shaped the early United States and
why and how historical interpretations can differ.
. Analyze primary and secondary sources related to slavery, expansion, and sectional conflict to understand
how different groups experienced and explained the Civil War and Reconstruction.
