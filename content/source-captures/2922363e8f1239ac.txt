Mission
The mission of the Bachelor of Science in Digital Marketing program is to
equip students with the essential knowledge, skills, and digital expertise
needed for a successful marketing career in a technology-driven world.
Program Learning Outcomes:
• Digital Marketing Strategies. Create strategies to engage audiences
across digital platforms.
• Marketing Technologies. Apply digital tools to optimize campaigns
and enhance consumer engagement.
• Content Creation. Develop compelling content that aligns brand
messaging with target audiences.
• Marketing Analytics. Analyze data to guide decisions and boost
marketing success.
• Ethics in Marketing. Apply ethical principles to marketing practices.
Degree Requirements
The bachelor’s degree requires a total of 120 credits, including major
coursework, General Education, and electives.
Students complete 39 credits of major coursework. Of these, three credits
also fulfill General Education requirements. As a result, the remaining
standalone General Education requirement is 39 credits.
The final 42 credits are completed through free electives, allowing
students the flexibility to tailor their academic experience.
