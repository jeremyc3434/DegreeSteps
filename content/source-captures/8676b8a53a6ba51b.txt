Accreditations & Recognitions
Institutional Accreditation
Excelsior University is an accredited institution and a member of the Middle States Commission on Higher Education (MSCHE or the Commission) www.msche.org . Excelsior University’s accreditation status is accreditation reaffirmed. The Commission’s most recent action on the institution’s accreditation status on June 23, 2022 was to reaffirm accreditation. MSCHE is recognized by the U.S. Secretary of Education to conduct accreditation and pre-accreditation (candidate status) activities for institutions of higher education including distance, correspondence education, and direct assessment programs offered at those institutions. The Commission’s geographic area of accrediting activities is throughout the United States.
Excelsior’s academic programs are registered by the New York State Education Department and/or have Florida-based licensing with the Commission for Independent Education, Florida Department of Education (License No. 12928).

FAQ
What subjects are typically included in a liberal arts degree program?
Excelsior University’s BS in Liberal Arts program includes courses in:
Creative problem-solving
Leadership and team building
Critical thinking
Mathematics
Psychology
Professional ethics
Public speaking
What skills can you develop in a BS in Liberal Arts program?
A Bachelor of Science in Liberal Arts can help you develop skills in:
Communication
Critical thinking
Problem-solving
Leadership
Research
What kinds of jobs can I get with a BS in Liberal Arts?
Graduates of Excelsior University’s BS in Liberal Arts program are prepared for a variety of careers, including education, military service, journalism, public relations, office administration, project coordination, and community outreach.
Are there concentrations or specializations within Excelsior University’s BS in Liberal Arts program?
Excelsior’s BS in Liberal Arts program offers Logistics Management as an optional concentration.
How does a liberal arts degree prepare students for leadership roles?
A liberal arts degree cultivates well-rounded individuals with the intellectual curiosity, critical thinking, communication skills, and ethical decision-making necessary to excel in leadership positions. The degree also primes you for the more advanced education you may need as your career progresses.
