Bachelor of Applied Science
Where Expertise Meets Real-World Management
Strayer University’s Bachelor of Applied Science in Management builds upon technical or allied health education by helping students develop management, business, and leadership capabilities that complement their existing expertise. The curriculum provides an opportunity to learn about key leadership skills including management, organizational behavior, human resources, project management, finance, marketing, accounting, and business strategy.
Designed for working adults and flexible learning formats, the program helps students understand how to lead people, manage projects, make business decisions, and improve organizational performance in workplaces increasingly shaped by technology, data, automation, and organizational change.
Students may have the opportunity to earn micro-credentials on the way to completing their degree.
Core Concepts
Apply management principles to technical and professional environments
Analyze business challenges and develop viable solutions
Lead teams and support organizational performance
Apply project management and operational practices
Understand finance, marketing, human resources, and organizational strategy
Practical Competencies
People leadership
Project management
Organizational behavior
Business decision making
Strategic planning
Financial literacy
Workforce management
Change management
Technology enabled management
Professional communication
Disclaimers
This degree is not specifically designed to prepare students for post-graduate study. Students planning to pursue a graduate degree should carefully review the entrance requirements of their selected graduate degree to ensure that the Bachelor of Applied Science in Management degree meets the undergraduate degree completion requirements for entry into the graduate degree program of their choice. Students desiring to pursue graduate- level study should consult with a dean, advisor or coach.
The Bachelor of Applied Science in Management program is available in an online-only format in Delaware, Maryland, North Carolina and Pennsylvania.
Strayer business programs create value for students by providing a flexible, innovative and career-focused experience to maximize their economic mobility and empower them to succeed in business. The Strayer University Bachelor of Applied Science in Management degree program is accredited by the Accreditation Council for Business Schools and Programs (ACBSP), 11520 West 119th Street, Overland Park, KS 66213, 913.339.9356.
Students are advised to review the Undergraduate Degree Conferral Requirements to ensure they satisfy all of the general requirements for an undergraduate degree, including the minimum grade that must be earned in certain courses.
