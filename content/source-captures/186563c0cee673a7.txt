Transfer credit opportunities for students who have completed prior college coursework
The legal studies program offers the opportunity to transfer substantial credit earned from prior college coursework for those who are eligible.
You can potentially transfer up to 90 approved credits from prior college coursework into the Bachelor of Science in Legal Studies program. This represents 74% of the total program semester credits ( 120 ), the time needed to complete the program, and cost of tuition.
Applying transfer credit is one of the most important parts of the application process, and our admissions advisors work hard to ensure you apply as much existing credit as possible into the legal studies program. Make sure you are prepared to submit all transcripts; you may be surprised which credits are transferable—so be sure to include everything.
If you have more specific questions about transferring credit from prior education , contact our admissions team and we can help guide you through the process.
