## Course substitutions by program

This course collection works for most TESU bachelor's programs. Some programs benefit from specific swaps in math and science. Find your situation below.

### BSBA (Accounting, Finance, Marketing, HR, General Management)

BSBA programs expect both algebra and statistics. Add 【23†Math 105: Precalculus Algebra】 (MAT-1210 College Algebra) alongside 【24†Statistics 101: Principles of Statistics】 to cover both.
### Computer Science and IT

If you're in a CS or IT program, you may need calculus instead of statistics for your quantitative requirement. Take 【25†Math 104: Calculus】 (MAT-2310 Calculus I) instead of Statistics 101.

TESU allows Computer Science courses to satisfy Category D, but courses that count toward your major can't also count for gen ed. If you're taking CS courses beyond your major requirements, those extras could fill your science slots instead of Biology 101 and Nutrition 101.
### Nursing (Accelerated BSN)

The science courses in the collection already work for BSN students. Nutrition 101 (BIO-2080) satisfies both a BSN admission prerequisite and a Category D gen ed requirement - TESU's 【26†pre-nursing page†www.tesu.edu】 confirms that nursing prerequisites can count toward gen ed.

Chemistry prerequisite: Study.com's chemistry courses do not satisfy the BSN chemistry prerequisite. You'll need to take General Chemistry at TESU or another provider.
### Liberal Studies and Non-Quantitative Programs (English, History, Communications, Psychology, Criminal Justice, Philosophy)

【27†Math 102: College Mathematics】 (MAT-1050 Applied Liberal Arts Mathematics) — Replace 【24†Statistics 101】. TESU designed MAT-1050 specifically for non-math majors. Not sufficient for BSBA or Computer Science programs.
### BA History

US History I (HIS-1130) and US History II (HIS-1140) count toward your area of study (major) requirements, not gen ed. You'll still need 6 credits of Category C History or Choice slots filled by other courses. Consider Western Civilization, World History, or additional Social Sciences or 【28†Humanities courses】 to fill those gen ed slots instead.
### Science-Focused BA Programs (e.g., BA Biology)

Your science area-of-study courses may overlap with Category D. If Biology 101 or Nutrition 101 already counts toward your major, it can't also count for gen ed - check your degree audit to see if you need different courses for your Category D slots.

## TESU general education course equivalencies FAQs

Do Study.com courses transfer to TESU?

Yes. TESU lists Study.com as an 【44†approved educational provider†www.tesu.edu】. Credits transfer via ACE recommendation. Verify your specific courses with a TESU advisor.

Which Study.com plan do I need?

All 14 online courses are on the College Saver plan ($95/mo).

What about SOS-1100?
SOS-1100 (Fact, Fiction, or Fake? Information Literacy Today) is a mandatory TESU course that cannot be transferred from any source. All TESU students must take it. The capstone course also must be taken at TESU.

Do I need both algebra and statistics?

For BSBA programs (Accounting, Management, Marketing, HR), TESU generally expects both. Other programs typically need only one quantitative course — 【24†Statistics 101】 alone covers the Quantitative Literacy requirement for a BA degree.
What is Edison Accelerate?

A $3,400 option that reduces your TESU residency to just 6 credits (SOS-1100 + Capstone). Ideal for students transferring in most of their degree from Study.com and other sources.

Does Psychology 101 count as a gen-ed at TESU?
No. Study.com's 【45†Psychology 101】 transfers as FEL-1999 (a free elective), not as PSY-1010. It does not satisfy the Category C Social Sciences requirement or any program prerequisite that requires PSY-1010. If you need 【46†psychology】 for your degree, take it at TESU.

I'm pursuing the BSN — which science courses should I prioritize?
Nutrition 101 (BIO-2080) and Biology 101 (BIO-1010) are already in the collection. Nutrition 101 satisfies both a BSN admission prerequisite and your Category D gen ed -- TESU allows nursing prerequisites to 【26†count toward gen ed†www.tesu.edu】. Study.com's chemistry courses do not satisfy the BSN chemistry prerequisite. You'll need to take General Chemistry elsewhere.

Is there a time limit on transfer credits?
TESU generally has no expiration on transfer credits. Business and technology courses older than 10 years may be evaluated for currency, but most gen-ed credits (humanities, social sciences, etc.) have no limit.
