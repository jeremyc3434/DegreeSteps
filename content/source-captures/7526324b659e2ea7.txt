Objective:
The course objective is to explain how societies have expressed ideas through art, music, literature, and philosophy from the ancient world through the 20th century as well as analyze how the past continues to shape culture, society, and everyday life today.
Learning Outcomes:
Compare works of literature from antiquity through the modern period, including works by historically marginalized voices
Compare transformations in European society, religion, and the arts to contemporaneous developments in other areas of the world
Compare major musical traditions from the medieval through the modern periods, identifying key composers, cultural values, and performance practices across Europe and beyond
Compare works of art and architecture from across cultures, locations, and historical periods
Explain major philosophical ideas from ancient Greece through the 20th century and their influence on society
Analyze how works of art, literature, and music have reflected, challenged, and shaped cultural beliefs from ancient Greece through the 20th century
