Resource Fees
The Resource Fees [also referred to as rEsource fees] are mandatory and encompass course electronic textbooks and materials, the
University library, eBook collection, virtual tutoring services, virtual science labs, the Centers for Math and Writing Excellence, Mic-
rosoft 365®, and access to Microsoft® Copilot and AI resources to support your learning. In limited circumstances, some courses
may require an additional textbook that is not covered by the Resource Fee. For a full listing of these University courses visit
https://www.phoenix.edu/courses/course-materials.html. Resource fees are not applicable to Competency-Based and Direct
Assessment programs.
