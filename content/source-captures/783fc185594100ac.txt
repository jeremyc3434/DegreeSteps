Objective:
The course objective is for learners to produce clear and compelling texts for academic, professional, and personal purposes that use effective style, structure, argumentation, and evidence.
Learning Outcomes:
Produce the components of an effective essay, including thesis statements, supporting arguments, structured paragraphs, and citations
Compose clear and logical texts using appropriate diction, style, and tone
Use and cite credible sources relevant to a topic using proper formatting
Use correct grammar, spelling, and punctuation in writing
Compose texts with clear organization and structure
Revise texts to improve clarity, persuasiveness, and appropriateness for audience and purpose
