COURSE DESCRIPTION
Sophia Learning's Macroeconomics course helps you explore and interpret the behavior of economies and
countries at both national and international levels. Through this exploration, you will learn how to evaluate
decisions on monetary and fiscal policy. You will also learn how to apply conceptual principles of
macroeconomics in practical ways to everyday life.
COURSE EFFECTIVE DATES: October 2025 - Present
PREREQUISITES: No prerequisites
LENGTH OF COURSE: This is a self-paced course. Students may use as much or as little time as needed to
complete the course.
ACE LEARNING EVALUATIONS RECOMMENDATION: In the lower-division baccalaureate category, 3 semester
hours in Principles of Macroeconomics (ACE ID: SOPH-0012).
GRADING: This is a pass/fail course. Students must complete 10 Challenges (formative assessments) and 5
Milestones (summative assessments) with an overall score of 70% or better.

LEARNING OUTCOMES
Upon completion of the course, the student will be able to:
. Describe the study of macroeconomics and economic indicators.
. Examine the principles of market efficiency, aggregate demand and supply, and fiscal policy.
. Analyze money, financial systems, and monetary policy.
. Interpret economic growth policies and international trade.
