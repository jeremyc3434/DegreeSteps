One locked-in price per course
We know every dollar matters, especially when you're balancing school with work, family and everything else. That’s why we offer our Locked-In Pricing Guarantee . Know what each course will cost before you enroll, and keep that price locked in — both tuition and the resource fee — for your enrolled program from start and finish. We are committed to making your costs predictable and providing the resources and support you need to keep moving toward your goals.
Resource fee includes
Course electronic textbooks and materials
Microsoft 365®, and access to Microsoft® Copilot and AI resources designed to support your learning
University Library and eBook collection
Virtual tutoring services
Center for Mathematics Excellence
Center for Writing Excellence
Virtual science labs
Additional services and support
Career Services for Life® including career coaching, resume building and interview preparation, as well as access to Career Navigator, a career guidance platform with assessments, planning tools, webinars and job search support — beginning on day one and throughout your lifetime as a graduate.
Programs built around skills employers want , with opportunities to add career-relevant skills to your professional profile every 5-6 weeks and earn badges that demonstrate your new knowledge
24/7 access to University resources for support
Locked-In Pricing Guarantee: Beginning November 2, 2026, all new students will be charged the same tuition rates and resource fees in effect for their enrolled program through their completion deadline unless they change programs. With the exception of any tuition and/or resource fee guarantee, the University may change its tuition or fees in its discretion.
Tuition Guarantee: Students enrolled prior to November 2, 2026 — or prior to January 4, 2027 for military students — will continue to pay their established tuition rate for their enrolled program unless they change programs. The Tuition Guarantee covers the tuition rate but does not include resource fees. With the exception of any tuition and/or resource fee guarantee, the University may change its tuition or fees in its discretion.
On rare occasion, additional fees may apply depending on your program, courses, assessments or services you use. A list of these fees are available in the Tuition and Fees section of the Academic Catalog . In limited circumstances, some courses may require an additional textbook that is not covered by the resource fee. View a full listing of these University courses.
Students can learn about their applicable program completion deadline and view their applicable guaranteed tuition rates and resource fees in their point of entry catalog and the current academic catalog, which are available in their student portal at https://my.phoenix.edu/academic-catalog.html .

How do I calculate my total cost of tuition?
The full cost of your program is based on several factors. This includes the number of credits per course—typically 3 credits but ranging from 1 to 6—and whether you have eligible transfer credits or qualify for other savings opportunities. But we’re here to help!
During the admissions process , you will receive an estimated personal financial plan based on the information you provide. Enrollment representatives are available to answer your questions.
You can get a head start by utilizing our Savings Explorer ® tool to help you estimate how much time and money you could potentially save on your degree.
Who is eligible for the Locked-In Pricing Guarantee?
All students who sign an enrollment agreement on or after November 2, 2026 (January 4, 2027 for military students) are eligible. The Locked-In Pricing Guarantee allows you to know the all-in cost of each course before you enroll and keep that price locked in for the entirety of your enrolled program.

What are my payment options? How will my payments work?
At the University of Phoenix, you have multiple options to pay for your education, giving you the flexibility to choose what works best for you. Potential options include:
Federal financial aid: Apply for grants, loans, or both by completing the FAFSA and meeting necessary requirements.
Military or Veterans Affairs benefits: Use benefits like the GI Bill ® or Tuition Assistance, with payments billed directly to the appropriate agency. [1]
Employer tuition assistance: Employers may cover some or all tuition costs for employees.
Tuition deferral: Delay tuition payments when using military benefits or employer reimbursement; uncovered fees must be paid upfront.
Third-party billing: The University bills approved third parties, such as employers, directly for tuition and fees.
Cash payment plans: Pay tuition upfront using credit cards, checks, or e-checks.
Private student loans: Borrow from private lenders to cover costs not met by federal aid, subject to credit and lender terms.
How your payments work will depend on the funding method you select. For example, payment schedules and processes may vary if you're using financial aid, employer assistance or a cash payment plan.
Learn more about your options for paying for school or connect with an enrollment representative who can guide you through the process and answer your specific questions.
[1] GI Bill® is a registered trademark of the U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs (VA). More information about education benefits offered by VA is available at the official U.S. government website at https://www.benefits.va.gov/gibill .
How can I save on my tuition?
Connect with an enrollment representative , who will work with you one-on-one to identify different opportunities that may be available to help you earn your degree faster and for less. You can also explore which savings opportunities you may be eligible for with our Savings Explorer ® tool .
Potential savings opportunities include:
Transfer eligible credits : If you’ve earned credits from an accredited institution, you may be able to transfer them to reduce the number of courses you need to take.
Credit for work and life experience : Through our Prior Learning Assessment (PLA), you can have your professional and life experience evaluated for potential college credit.
Scholarships : Apply for our competition-free scholarships, which can help lower your out-of-pocket costs.
Employer benefits : Check if your employer offers tuition assistance or reimbursement programs.
Self-paced courses : Complete low-cost, self-paced courses from alternative course providers and apply those credits toward your degree.
Testing for credit : Demonstrate your existing knowledge by taking a test from one of the acceptable National Testing Programs such as CLEP, DSST, AP and many more from credit that may be applied to your degree program.
Military credits : All branches of the military have had military experience assessed through the American Council of Education (ACE). The University honors these credit recommendations, and those credits can be applied to your undergrad program at University of Phoenix.
Community college benefits : Leverage agreements between the University of Phoenix and select community colleges across the U.S. to save on your education.
Special tuition rates : Associate degree graduates, alumni and military students are eligible for lower tuition rates.
By exploring these options, you can find a flexible, affordable pathway to earning your degree.
Do I qualify for a special tuition rate?
In addition to our Locked-In Pricing Guarantee , some students may be eligible for special tuition rates:
Associate degree graduates: As a graduate from an accredited college or university, you have access to a special tuition rate on your bachelor’s degree.
Alumni: You can take advantage of an exclusive alumni offer to save on another degree.
Military students: If you are affiliated with the U.S. Armed Forces, as active-duty service members and family members of active duty and selected reserve, you may be eligible for a lower, special tuition rate . We also waive resource fees for qualifying undergraduate service members.
Please note that students who receive a reduced tuition rate in one of the categories described above will not be eligible for any other University tuition and/or fee reduction, waiver, benefit or offer, subject to the exception above for military students. If a student wishes to use any other University tuition and/or fee reduction, waiver, benefit or offer, it will only be applied to the University's standard tuition rate. For more information, please reach out to an enrollment representative.
What resources can help me navigate paying for school?
The University of Phoenix offers a variety of resources to help you make informed financial decisions about your education:
Savings Explorer: Identify opportunities to save on your education costs.
Scholarships: Review potential scholarship opportunities that may be available.
Financial Literacy and Wellness Center: Access tools and resources to build smart money management skills.
Paying for School: Explore funding options and strategies to reduce tuition costs.
Financial Aid: Learn about available federal aid opportunities and browse answers to common related questions .
Tuition-related blog articles: Get insights and tips on tuition planning , financial aid and an array of savings opportunities.
These resources provide general information to help you plan and manage your tuition effectively.

Our enrollment representatives are here to walk you through your payment options, as well as savings opportunities, including scholarships, employer tuition benefits and more. Connect with a representative today so we can help you determine the best options for your specific situation.
