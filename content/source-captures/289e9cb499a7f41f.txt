Objective:
This course is designed as both a stand-alone course that will be necessary to proceed into more advanced managerial accounting topics and as an advanced companion to Sophia Learning's Financial Accounting course. This course utilizes extended and recurring examples to illustrate managerial accounting theory and practice. Students frequently refer to live spreadsheets that show calculations and formulas in action, while interactive prompts cue students to try running calculations for themselves prior to completing assessments. Accounting concepts are differentiated for manufacturing, merchandising, and service companies and real-world case studies are interspersed to provide further context for accounting theory.
Learning Outcomes:
Apply cost concepts and financial accounting concepts to managerial accounting.
Analyze the use of accounting for quality and cost management.
Analyze the use of accounting information in decision-making and budget setting.
Compare the use and analysis of the common costing systems.
General Topics:
Applying Financial Accounting Concepts to Managerial Accounting
Managerial Accounting Careers and Ethics
Reporting Production, Materials, Labor, and Overhead costs
Job Order Costing and Analysis

Variable and Absorption Costing Analysis and Decision Making

Direct and Indirect Methods for Cash Flows
Cost Behavior: Fixed, Variable, and Mixed
Cost Volume, Break-even, Sales Mix and Target Profit Analysis
Master and Product Cost Budgeting
Standard Costing and Variances
Decision Making with Differential Analysis

Responsibility Accounting
