Build on Learning From Work, Service, and Experience
Learning does not only happen in a college classroom. You may have gained college-level knowledge through military service, professional training, certifications, exams, or other structured learning experiences. UMass Global offers several ways to review eligible prior learning. The option that fits you depends on the learning you completed and the program you want to pursue.

ACE-Recommended Learning
The American Council on Education, or ACE, reviews certain learning completed outside a traditional college classroom. UMass Global accepts eligible ACE credit recommendations, subject to program requirements and other academic policies. ACE may include recommendations for:
Military courses, training, and occupations
Employer and workforce training
Professional and technical learning
Approved alternative courses
Some examinations
Use the ACE National Guide to search for your course, training, credential, or exam. Then apply to UMass Global and submit documentation for review.
Search the ACE National Guide
ACE recommendations can help you understand what may be reviewed. UMass Global determines how eligible credit applies toward your selected degree.
Continue Your Education With Sophia or Study.com
Have you completed courses through Sophia or Study.com? Eligible coursework may help you make progress toward a UMass Global degree. Eligible students may also receive a 10-20% tuition discount , depending on their program.

Credit by Exam
You may be able to earn credit for knowledge you have already demonstrated. UMass Global accepts eligible scores from:
Advanced Placement (AP)
College Level Examination Program (CLEP)
DANTES Subject Standardized Tests (DSST)
International Baccalaureate (IB)
Defense Language Proficiency Examinations (DLAT/DLPT)
Subject matter specific exams from institutionally accredited providers
Selected UMass Global credit-by-exam opportunities
Credit depends on the exam, your score, and current UMass Global requirements. In some cases, eligible credit may meet general education, major, or elective requirements. Credits are typically lower division unless specified as equivalent to an upper division course.

Credit for Military Training and Experience
Your military service, coursework, training, and experience may help you make progress toward a UMass Global degree. For undergraduate programs, UMass Global reviews military learning using ACE credit evaluation guidelines.
Undergraduate military students receive 6 credits for military service. Additional credit may be awarded based on ACE recommendations.
For graduate programs, military coursework may be considered by programs that accept it. Requirements vary by program.
What you may need:
Military education or training records
Joint Services Transcript, if applicable
Previous college transcripts
Your intended UMass Global program

Prior Learning Assessment (PLA) by Portfolio
Some undergraduate students may be able to earn credit for learning gained through work, life, or other non-college experiences. Through a portfolio, you can show how your learning meets the objectives of a UMass Global course.
Portfolio credit is awarded for demonstrated learning—not for a job title or years of experience. A separate portfolio is required for each 3-credit course, and a maximum of 30 credits may be awarded through portfolio assessment.
For more information, review the Prior Learning Assessment by Portfolio Policy or speak with an enrollment coach.

Frequently Asked Questions
What is Credit for Prior Learning?
Credit for Prior Learning may help you earn credit for learning completed outside a traditional college course. This may include military training, professional certifications, workforce training, qualifying exams, ACE-recommended learning, or a prior learning portfolio.
UMass Global reviews your documentation to determine whether credit may apply to your program.
What do I need to submit for a CPL review?
What you submit depends on the type of learning you want reviewed. You may need transcripts, exam scores, certification records, training documents, military records, or a Joint Services Transcript.
Apply to UMass Global and send any available documents. Your enrollment coach can help you understand what to submit.
Is Credit for Prior Learning guaranteed?
No. Credit depends on your documentation, your program requirements, and current UMass Global policies. Eligible credit may apply to general education, major, or elective requirements, depending on the review.
