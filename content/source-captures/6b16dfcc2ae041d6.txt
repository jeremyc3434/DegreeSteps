I. PURPOSE
Western Governors University's (WGU) Residency Requirements and Credentialing policy establishes the minimum requirements to be completed at WGU.
Scope : This policy applies to all students enrolling in or pursuing any credential at WGU.

III. DEFINITIONS
Competency : A measurable, assessable capability of an individual to successfully complete activities and perform tasks at a determined level, ranging from fundamental awareness to advanced application of knowledge and skills.
Competency Unit : A measurement of student knowledge awarded to a student for successfully completing all assessments associated with a course. The unit is used at WGU to measure completion of program requirements. As a measure of equivalence with other institutions, one Competency Unit is equal to one Semester Credit of learning.
Credential : An achievement awarded by a responsible and authorized body (i.e., an institution of higher education) that has specific learning outcomes or competency demonstration that aligns to a given standard or standards (accreditation standards or professional or organizational standards and frameworks).
Degree : A credential conferred by a college, university, or other postsecondary institution as official recognition for successfully completing a program of study. Degrees may contain stacked courses, certificates, micro-credentials, etc.
Micro-credential : A form of credential endorsed by the issuing institution to verify, validate, and attest an individual has demonstrated specific outcomes. Micro-credentials are comprised of sequences of achievements that usually align with employer and industry needs. Micro-credentials may stand alone, be stackable, or be embedded in a larger credential(s). WGU recognizes the following micro-credentials:
Certificate: Prepares learners for a particular job or set of professional or academic skills. While similar in scope and targeted outcomes to the certification, certificates are not industry-recognized qualifications. Certificates are a standalone, embedded, or concentration of a degree program.
Specialization: Prepares learners for various specified jobs and skills within a professional sector or academic domain. Specializations are a sub-component track or path of a degree program. Specializations are a standalone, embedded, or concentration of a degree program.
Stackable : The ability to combine offerings ( competencies, courses, micro-credentials , certificate /degree programs ) towards aggregated credentials.
Term : A six-month period, beginning on the first of the month and ending on the final day of the sixth month, during which students may access and complete their coursework.

IV. POLICY
Minimum Program & Micro-Credential Completion Requirements:
WGU recognizes and/or awards digital credentials at the Degree and Micro-credential levels.
Residency requires students to complete some portion of the Degree Competency Units (CUs) earned directly within the WGU program the student is actively pursuing, in addition to any required capstone course(s) or clinical(s). Individual schools and/or programs may impose more stringent residency requirements than outlined in this policy.
Minimum Residency Requirements. To receive a Degree from WGU, students must meet the minimum residency requirements outlined below.
General Residency Conditions (applicable to all degree levels):
Only competency units earned within the active WGU program may count toward residency.
Competency units must receive a Passed grade to apply toward residency.
Competency Units earned in previously completed WGU programs do not count toward residency. An exception may be granted only through an approved Stackable credential pathway, as described in Section IV.6.
Transfer credit awarded for embedded industry certifications within the active WGU program may be applied toward residency only with an academic review and approval from the Office of the Registrar.
Degree-Specific Residency Requirements:
For undergraduate degree programs, students must earn at least 25% of the required Degree Competency Units directly from the program the student is actively pursuing at WGU.
For graduate degree programs, students must earn at least 50% of the required Degree Competency Units directly from the program the student is actively pursuing at WGU.
Minimum Micro-Credential Completion Requirements:
Students must earn all the required Micro-credential Competency Units from WGU .
To earn a Micro-Credential at WGU, WGU may award credit for verified Competencies already completed at WGU and accept transfer credit for third-party Certifications.
WGU will only award course credit(s) for digital credentials if there is a 100% competency alignment.
When a student earns a subsequent credential at WGU, some or all verified competencies from a previously completed WGU credential may count toward the residency requirement only if the credentials are part of an approved Stackable credential pathway.
Competency Units earned from prior WGU program versions will be evaluated and may, at WGU's discretion, satisfy requirements for the current program version.
If a student earns and is issued a digital credential as part of a WGU non-degree offering, WGU will not reissue the same or equivalent digital credential if the student completes another program in which the same or equivalent credential is included. However, course credit can be received for successful transfer students.

V. PROCEDURES
Should a student desire to appeal decisions based on the Residency Requirements and Credentialing policy, the student should submit an appeal. Enrolling, prospective students should connect with their Enrollment Counselor to initiate the Credential Evaluation Appeal process. Active students pursuing a credential at WGU should submit an appeal with the title "Residency Requirement Appeal" detailing the decision desired by the student and the reasons that WGU should accept the decision to the Office of the Registrar by emailing [email protected] .
