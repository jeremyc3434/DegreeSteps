BS in Business, Business Administration
School of Business, Technology, and Health Care
Administration
The BS in Business, Business Administration
specialization provides students with opportunities to
develop and demonstrate the business, interpersonal,
and professional knowledge, skills, and abilities needed
to contribute to organizational effectiveness across all
functional levels in manufacturing, service,
professional, and government arenas. Students select
elective courses based on their individual professional
needs.
