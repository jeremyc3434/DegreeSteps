Mission
The mission of the Bachelor of Science in Human Resources program
is to equip students with the essential knowledge and skills needed for
successful careers in human resource management.
Program Learning Outcomes:
• Strategic Human Resources: Create people strategies that support
organizational mission and goals.
• Human Resource Technology and Analytics: Utilize data and
technology to make informed HR decisions.
• Workforce Planning: Develop programs for attracting, retaining, and
nurturing talent while applying ethical principles and legal standards.
• Employee Experience: Create people and organizational development
initiatives to enhance efficiency and effectiveness and promote
inclusion and equity.
• Rewards Management: Demonstrate a comprehensive understanding
of the principles and practices related to employee compensation and
benefits.
Degree Requirements
The bachelor’s degree requires a total of 120 credits, including major
coursework, General Education, and electives.
Students complete 45 credits of major coursework. Of these, three credits
also fulfill General Education requirements. As a result, the remaining
standalone General Education requirement is 39 credits.
The final 36 credits are completed through free electives, allowing
students the flexibility to tailor their academic experience.
