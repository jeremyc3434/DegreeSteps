COURSE DESCRIPTION
Sophia Learning’s Introduction to Nutrition course introduces general concepts and practical applications of
nutrition. The course reflects an integration of the science of nutrition and how food provides nutrients for
healthy living and disease management throughout the lifespan. An emphasis is given to the role of nutrients in
digestion, absorption, and metabolism. Food safety and availability, food security, dietary supplements, food
labels, and marketing are also addressed in this course.
COURSE EFFECTIVE DATES: September 2025 - Present
PREREQUISITES: No prerequisites
LENGTH OF COURSE: This is a self-paced course. Students may use as much or as little time as needed to
complete the course.
ACE LEARNING EVALUATIONS RECOMMENDATION: In the lower-division baccalaureate category, 3 semester
hours in Introduction to Nutrition (ACE ID: SOPH-0063).
GRADING: This is a pass/fail course. Students must complete 17 Challenges (formative assessments) and 5
Milestones (summative assessments) with an overall score of 70% or better.
