Locked In Pricing Guarantee
Beginning November 2, 2026, all new students will be charged the same tuition rates and resource fees in effect for their enrolled
program through their completion deadline unless they change programs. A program change does not include updating to a newer
version of the same program; updating to an en-route, certificate track, or state-specific variation of the same program; or re-enter-
ing the same program. With the exception of any tuition and/or resource fee guarantee, the University may change its tuition or fees
in its discretion.
Students who have tuition and/or resource fees guaranteed at a previous rate can view their applicable guaranteed tuition rates and
resource fees in the Tuition Rates Prior to 11/2/2026 section of the catalog, or in their point of entry catalog (available in their stu-
dent portal at https://my.phoenix.edu/academic-catalog.html).
