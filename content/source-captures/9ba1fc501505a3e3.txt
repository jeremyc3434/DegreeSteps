Online Courses
Online Testing
At Thomas Edison State University, online courses are one of many ways to earn credit and use approaches designed specifically with the adult learner in mind.
How Online Courses Work
Thomas Edison State University’s online courses are vibrant, engaging classes you will access though myEdison Experience, our online course management system. The courses are guided by mentors on a 12- or 8-week timeline, and if exams are required, you will take proctored exams via the Online Proctor Service (OPS).

Logging In
You will receive a login ID and password by email before the course begins. Then, you will log in for your online classroom at a time and place that suits your schedule. Each course has a required number of times per week that students must log in (average is three times a week), but never at any set time. myEdison Experience uses the Moodle learning management system through which you will submit your assignments and have class discussions. You will find your interactions with your mentor and classmates to be lively and thoughtful — like actually being in a classroom together.
