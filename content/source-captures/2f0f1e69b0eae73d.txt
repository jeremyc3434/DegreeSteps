Bachelor of Science, Software Engineering
The Bachelor of Science in Software Engineering prepares students to design, build, and maintain modern software
systems while developing strong problem-solving and leadership skills. The program emphasizes practical, project-
based learning in areas such as programming, algorithms, cloud computing, and software design, while also covering
essential topics like security, databases, and Agile development. Upon completion of the program, graduates are
equipped to pursue careers as software engineers, designers, and developers.
