Objective:
The course objective is to provide a continuation of Spanish I by introducing students to more complex verb tenses and grammatical structures. This course offers students the opportunity to build on their Spanish speaking, reading, and writing skills through both a linguistic and cultural lens, exploring the cultures and customs of several Spanish-speaking countries along with new vocabulary and sentence constructions for discussing home and work routines, travel and recreation, and events that have happened in both the recent and more distant past. It is highly recommended that students complete Spanish I prior to taking this course.
Learning Outcomes:
Express preferences regarding travel and recreation, state phrases useful for tourism and seasonal activities, and use the preterite tense to describe past experiences.
Describe daily chores and responsibilities, specify useful shopping expressions and pricing information, and express oneself in a work environment.
Identify personal relationships, state appropriate terminology for digital communication, and use the preterite and imperfect tenses to summarize current events and describe health and medical incidents.
Recognize major cultural aspects of the Spanish-speaking world, including customs and traditions, significant locations, and other facets of daily life.
