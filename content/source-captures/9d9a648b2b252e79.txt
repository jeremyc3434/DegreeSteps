COURSE DESCRIPTION
Principles of Management is designed to introduce students to the four functions of management: planning,
organizing, leading, and controlling. This course describes the skills and techniques required by a manager to
perform these functions. Specifically, students will learn about the stages of decision making, strategic analysis,
ethical business strategies, goal setting, communication skills, and global awareness.

Touchstone 4: Organization Analysis & Strategic Recommendations (150 points): Students will select an
organization they are familiar with or have worked for (currently or in the past). The student is tasked with
analyzing the organization both internally and externally using some of the tools and frameworks they have
learned in this course. The student is also tasked with recommending changes to the organization’s
strategy and structure, in addition to considering associated ethical implications. This assignment requires
the student to apply SWOT analysis, PESTEL analysis, Porter’s Five Forces of Industry Analysis, Porter’s
Generic Strategies, and organization design concepts related to strategy-structure fit. Students will prepare
a slide deck containing their analysis and recommendations that could be presented to the organization’s
Board.
