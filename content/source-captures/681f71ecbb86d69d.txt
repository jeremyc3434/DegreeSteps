COURSE DESCRIPTION
Sophia Learning's English Composition I course is designed to build core skills in English Composition and
teach you how to apply those skills to become an effective writer. You will gain proficiency in the usage of basic
grammar, punctuation, and mechanics, as well as learn how to construct effective sentences, paragraphs, and
essays. Additionally, you will navigate the writing process to develop a variety of different composition types.
COURSE EFFECTIVE DATES: April 2024 - Present
PREREQUISITES: No prerequisites
LENGTH OF COURSE: This is a self-paced course. Students may use as much or as little time as needed to
complete the course.
ACE LEARNING EVALUATIONS RECOMMENDATION: In the lower-division baccalaureate category, 3 semester
hours in English Composition I (ACE ID: SOPH-0015).
GRADING: This is a pass/fail course. Students must complete 14 Challenges (formative assessments) and 5
Touchstones (project-based or written assessments) with an overall score of 70% or better.

Touchstones are projects that illustrate comprehension of the course material, help refine skills, and
demonstrate application of knowledge. Read further for information on the touchstones in this course:
Touchstone 1: Narrative Essay (50 points): Students will write a 500-800 words narrative essay telling a
short, interesting personal story.
Touchstone 2.1: Comparison/Contrast Essay (50 points): Students will write a 3-4 page (approximately 700-
1000 words) comparison/contrast essay in the informative mode.
Touchstone 2.2: Informative Essay Draft (40 points): Students will draft a 3-5 page (approximately 800-
1300 words) informative essay. As a part of their completed draft, students will also complete the color
coding activity.
Touchstone 3: Informative Essay Revision (60 points): Students will review the in-text comments and
summary feedback they received on their Touchstone 2.2 draft to deepen and extend their analysis of their
topic. Students will submit a revision of their Touchstone 2.2 draft that reflects the feedback.
