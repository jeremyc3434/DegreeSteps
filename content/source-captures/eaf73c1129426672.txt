Is CLI fully accredited?
Christian Leaders Institute holds "Candidate" status with the Association for Biblical Higher Education Commission on Accreditation, 5850 T. G. Lee Blvd., Ste. 130, Orlando, FL 32822, 407.207.0808. Candidate status is a pre-accreditation status granted to those institutions that meet the ABHE Conditions of Eligibility and that possess such qualities as may provide a basis for achieving accreditation status within five years.
Click here to view Christian Leaders Institute on ABHE’s directory.
Click here to view Christian Leaders Institute on the US Department of Education’s Database of Accredited Postsecondary Institutions and Programs.
We also have recognition out of the state of Florida to issue religious degrees.
For more information, you can view that by clicking here ( https://web02.fldoe.org/CIE/Religious/ReligiousInstitutions.aspx ) .
Finally, we also have non-governmentally-recognized accreditation with IABCS ( https://iabcs.net/ )
