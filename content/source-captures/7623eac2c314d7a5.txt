Objective:
The course objective is to prepare students for the skills needed for Calculus I. In this course, students build off the topics in Sophia’s College Algebra course by evaluating and graphing functions and solving systems of equations using matrices. Students then investigate different types of functions by graphing power, polynomial, and rational functions. By solving exponential and logarithmic equations, they model real-world situations. Then, they move into the trigonometry portion of Precalculus by analyzing trigonometric functions through graphs, equations, and identities and solving oblique triangles using the law of sines and the law of cosines. Students apply trigonometric concepts to the polar coordinates system and the trigonometric form of complex numbers. Finally, the course wraps up with supplementary topics which include vectors, writing equations of conic sections, working with arithmetic and geometric sequences and series, and expanding powers of binomials.
Learning Outcomes:
Analyze trigonometric functions through graphs, equations, and identities.
Graph power, polynomial, and rational functions and model real-world situations.
Write equations of conic sections, write the nth term and find the sum of arithmetic and geometric sequences, and expand powers of binomials.
Evaluate and graph functions and solve systems of equations.
Find derivatives of functions and apply the differentiation process when appropriate.
