UNDERGRADUATE CREDIT
HOUR AND MYPATH DEGREE
REQUIREMENTS
Graduation Requirements–Associate
Degrees
• A minimum of 60 credits is required.
• Completion of degree program requirements.
• 15 credits in residence.
• 2.0 or equivalent minimum university of Massachusetts Global
cumulative grade point average. Programs may have additional grade
requirements,
• Resolution of all incomplete grades.
• Degree requirements cannot be waived by the CSU or IGETC
certification (CA), California General Education Transfer Curriculum
(Cal-GETC), Washington and Oregon state-approved Direct Transfer
degrees, Associate Degrees for Transfer or other state’s equivalent
transfer certification.
• LBSU 105 Academic Foundations / LBSC 105 Academic
Foundations is required in the Associate of Arts in General Education.
• LBSC 304 Liberal Arts Core Foundations / LBSU 304 Liberal Arts Core
Foundations is required in the Associate of Arts in General Business.
Graduation Requirements–Bachelor
Degrees
• A minimum of 120 credits.
• 36 credits earned in upper-division coursework.
• Minimum of 30 credits completed in residence at University of
Massachusetts Global; 18 of which must be upper-division credits, 12
of which must be completed in students major.
• Completion of degree program requirements.
• A 2.0 or equivalent minimum grade point average on a 4.0 scale at
University of Massachusetts Global.
• A 2.0 or equivalent minimum grade point average in all major and
minor coursework taken at University of Massachusetts Global.
Programs may have additional grade requirements.
• Resolution of all incomplete grades.
• LBSU 302 Information Fluency and Academic Integrity / LBSC 302
Information Fluency & Academic Integrity (Cannot be used as an
elective in a major, minor, or certificate program)
• LBSU 304 Liberal Arts Core Foundations / LBSC 304 Liberal Arts Core
Foundations (Cannot be used as an elective in a major, minor, or
certificate program)
Academic Major Credits and specific
courses
• See program listing. Students must follow general education and
major requirements of the same year.
Undergraduate Credit Hour and MyPath Degree Requirements 1
Other Requirements
• A minimum of 21 credits of upper-division coursework in the major;
• A 2.0 or equivalent University of Massachusetts Global
cumulative grade point average on a 4.0 scale in the major and in
upper-division work.
Double Major (Seeking Concurrently)
• A maximum of 18 credits may be duplicated by the second major.
• Completion of all requirements for both majors.
• Diploma(s) will be issued for each type of degree showing the majors
completed at the time of graduation.
Minors
• Must be completed in a discipline outside the student’s major.
• A minimum of 18 credits, 9 of which, may not be duplicated by the
major.
• A minimum of 9 upper-division credits.
• A minimum of 6 upper-division credits completed in residence.
• 2.0 or equivalent University of Massachusetts Global cumulative
grade point average and 2.0 GPA for all upper-division coursework.
Programs may have additional grade requirements.
Multiple Emphases or Concentrations
There is no limitation to the number of emphasis or concentration areas
a student may complete within their degree program. At least one 3-unit
emphasis or concentration course must be unduplicated within the other
emphasis areas or concentrations.
Students should consult with their advisor and One Stop regarding
degree and financial implications of completing multiple emphases or
concentrations.
Electives
• Course of the student’s own choosing taken beyond requirements of
general education and major/minor in order to meet minimum credit
requirements for graduation.
Second or Additional Bachelor’s Degree at
University of Massachusetts Global
(After initial Bachelors degree has been conferred)
• A student must apply and be admitted to seek a second bachelor
degree or major.
• All major requirements must be met.
• A minimum of 30 semester credits in residence beyond the minimum
requirements for the first bachelor’s degree.
• LBSU 100 Student Success Strategies / LBSC 100 Student
Success Strategies, LBSU 302 Information Fluency and Academic
Integrity / LBSC 302 Information Fluency & Academic Integrity and
LBSU 304 Liberal Arts Core Foundations / LBSC 304 Liberal Arts Core
Foundationsare waived for all bachelor degree holders from another
institutionally accredited institution.
