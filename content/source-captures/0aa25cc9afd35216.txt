Christian Leaders Institute Catalog
What courses or programs interest you? The Christian Leaders Institute Catalog features the available college courses and programs. This web page is your index or sitemap to the catalog pages.
Christian Leaders Institute is accessed when you sign up for an account with Christian Leaders Institute, Institute or Alliance.
The Christian Leaders Institute courses are accessed online and students have 180 days to complete the courses. These free courses feature accredited professors and free study materials with tests, quizzes, and papers in some courses.
Christian Leaders Institute provides tuition-free courses and Low fee credentials for bi-vocational or career minister training.
