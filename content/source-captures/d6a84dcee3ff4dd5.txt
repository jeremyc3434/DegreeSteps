Unlock the Future with Data Science and AI
Champlain College’s online Bachelor’s in Data Science and Artificial Intelligence prepares you to excel in today’s data-driven world. Designed for innovators, this program integrates computer science, mathematics, and AI methodologies to give you the skills needed to lead in fields ranging from machine learning to data analytics. With an emphasis on cutting-edge tools and real-world applications, this program will propel your career in data analytics by leveraging and modifying the abilities of AI.
Finish your program faster with credit for prior learning and experience .
Differentiate your degree and get your resume noticed with an added certificate or graduate courses . Speak with your advisor for information.
Consider a career using the skills you learn in this degree.
Innovate with Data Science and AI
Mathematical and Computational Foundations: Build a strong base in linear algebra, calculus, and discrete mathematics, essential for complex problem-solving in AI and data science.
Proficiency in Programming and Algorithm Development: Master Python, R, and SQL programming languages to create efficient algorithms and data analysis solutions.
Data Management and Analysis Skills: Gain expertise in relational and NoSQL databases, enabling you to design, manage, and query large datasets for data-driven decisions.
Specialization in AI and Machine Learning: Learn to implement and optimize machine learning models, including neural networks and computer vision applications, to drive innovation in the use of artificial intelligence.
Critical Thinking and Problem-Solving in AI: Apply theoretical and practical AI knowledge to solve industry challenges and innovate responsibly within data science.

MATH 190
Linear Algebra and Analytical Geometry
3 Credits
learn more
This course covers the fundamental concepts of linear algebra and analytical geometry, including matrices, vectors, linear transformations, and systems of linear equations. Students will also explore the analytical geometry of Euclidean spaces, including lines, planes, circles, spheres, and conic sections. In this course, students will develop their mathematical reasoning skills and learn how to apply these principles to solve real-world problems.
Click to Open
MATH 210
Calculus I
3 Credits
learn more
Students will explore foundational concepts of calculus as they investigate limits, derivatives, and integrals, applying the product, quotient, and chain rules for differentiation and integration to algebraic, exponential, logarithmic, and trigonometric functions. Through problem-solving and real-world applications, students will analyze and optimize functions, sketch curves, and compute areas under graphs for both definite and indefinite integrals, and integrate by substitution and by parts.
Click to Open
MATH 270
Discrete Mathematics
3 Credits
learn more
This course introduces foundational concepts in discrete mathematics. Students will explore set theory, logic, and Boolean algebra, apply proof techniques like induction, and solve combinatorics problems using permutations and combinations. The course emphasizes graph theory, including trees, Euler and Hamilton paths, and algorithms, and examines functions and growth measures such as Big-O notation to develop critical problem-solving skills.
Prerequisites
Complete MATH-210

Get a head start on your master's degree
Bachelor's to Master's Accelerated Pathway
Thinking about a master’s degree? As a data science and artificial intelligence bachelor’s student, you can take up to 9 graduate credits within your general electives, billed at the undergraduate tuition rate, and those credits count toward your master’s so you finish sooner and spend less. Use those credits to earn a graduate certificate aligned with your intended master’s, or, if no certificate fits, take the first courses from your master’s program. Visit the Bachelor's to Masters Pathway page for details, and connect with your academic success advisor early to make sure your courses apply.

Accelerate Your Degree
Time to Completion
A 120-credit bachelor's degree in data science and artificial intelligence traditionally takes 4 years to complete. Your time to completion is dependent on how many courses you take per term and whether you have credits to transfer.
CCO accepts up to 90 transfer credits toward a bachelor's degree. If you transfer the maximum, you could have as few as 10 courses (about 5 terms) left to finish. The average CCO bachelor's student brings in 40 credits. That leaves about 80 credits, or 27 courses, to complete. If you take two classes per 7-week term , that works out to just over 2 years on average to earn a bachelor’s degree . Your own timeline may be shorter or longer, depending on your situation.
We try to make online learning as seamless and quick as you want it to be. Because we believe, no matter where you've started, CCO is where you can finish strong – and save time and tuition along the way.

Frequently Asked Questions
Following are some of the questions we are asked about our bachelor's degree in data science and artificial intelligence.
Is there an associate degree I can earn prior to pursuing this bachelor's degree?
This bachelor's program is designed as the natural next step after Champlain's Associate of Science in Data Science, and your transfer credit team can help map how prior coursework applies. Work with your our admission team or your student success advisor to determine what your path should be.
What AI topics are part of this bachelor's degree?
The AI course sequence covers machine learning fundamentals, popular ML frameworks and model deployment, deep learning and neural network architectures (including CNNs and RNNs), computer vision (image processing, object detection, facial recognition), and natural language processing (text preprocessing, sentiment analysis, machine translation).
What is a bachelor's in data science and artificial intelligence?
It's a 100% online, 120-credit bachelor's degree that integrates computer science, mathematics, and AI methodologies. It's designed to prepare you for careers spanning machine learning, data analytics, and AI applications across tech-driven industries.
How quickly can I complete this degree?
Most students finish in about 3 years and 6 months, taking two courses per term. Actual time to completion depends on how many transfer credits you bring in. Note that some courses in this program are only available in 15-week terms, so it's worth talking with an advisor about how that could affect your timeline.
How many courses am I able to take each term?
As you pursue your associate degree in data science, you may take 1 course, or you may take 2-3 courses per each 7-week term. However, you may not take more than three courses (9 credit hours) in a term, or 6 courses (18 credit hours) in a 15-week semester without prior program director approval. Be aware that the 15 week courses in this program will also affect the number of courses you can complete each term. As always, consult your Student Success Advisor with any questions.
