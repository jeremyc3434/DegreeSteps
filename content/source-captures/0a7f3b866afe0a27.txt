Bachelor of Science, Information Technology Management
The Bachelor of Science in Information Technology Management is a comprehensive, competency-based program
designed to equip graduates for leadership and management roles in the dynamic field of information technology.
With a strong emphasis on overseeing and directing digital transformations, the program prepares students for
advanced positions by combining core business management skills with in-depth technological competencies.
Graduates will be adept in data-driven decision-making, product lifecycle management, systems analysis, and
identifying and leveraging emerging trends. This curriculum positions learners for modern and strategic roles such as
Director of Innovation and Technology, Information Systems Manager, or Software Product Owner, providing the skills
necessary to manage digital product portfolios, lead IT projects, consult on technological strategies, and drive digital
innovation across various industries including technology firms, healthcare, finance, education, and government.
