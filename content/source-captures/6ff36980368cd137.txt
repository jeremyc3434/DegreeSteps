Transferring from another school?
Empire State University is one of the most transfer-friendly schools in the world.
Approximately 90% of our students arrive with transfer credits, and more than half
of those students attended at least two other institutions.
Most institutions only accept up to 60 transfer credits (and are selective about it).
All of your lower-division transfer credits can be accepted by SUNY Empire. If you
are earning a bachelor’s degree, up to 79 of those credits can count toward your degree.
Have college credits you’ve already earned?
See how they can bring you closer to your SUNY Empire degree. Our Credit Transfer Tool makes it easy to find out which of your previous courses and credits may transfer.
In just a few minutes, you’ll get a clear view of how much progress you’ve already
made toward your degree—and how close you are to finishing.
Take the next step with confidence. Your experience counts at SUNY Empire.

How Do You Get Transfer Credits?
There is no special application for transfer students. That means no extra work for
you.
Just submit your official transcripts for any credits you wish to transfer when you
apply to SUNY Empire.
You can send your transcripts physically or electronically.
For physical transcripts, send to this address:
Empire State University
Office of Admissions
111 West Avenue
Saratoga Springs, NY 12866
If sending electronically, send official transcripts to Admissions@sunyempire.edu .

Transfer Credit Details
Associate Degree Transfer Credit
Up to 40 credits can be transferred (64 credits required for associate degree; 24
credits must be taken at SUNY Empire)
Credits with letter grade of C- and above are eligible
Bachelor's Degree Transfer Credit
Up to 93 credits can be transferred (124 credits required for bachelor’s degree; 31
credits must be taken at SUNY Empire)
Credits with letter grade of C- and above are eligible
If part of a completed associate degree, credits with a passing letter grade (D or
better) may be eligible
Students need 45 upper division credits for their baccalaureate degree
Master's Degree Transfer Credit
Up to 12 graduate-level credits can be transferred (some programs set lower limits)
Credits with a letter grade of B or better are eligible
Credits must be less than six years old at the time of admission and must logically
fit into your degree program
Doctoral Degree Transfer Credit
Up to 6 graduate-level credits can be transferred
Credits with a letter grade of B or better are eligible
Credits must be less than six years old at the time of admission and must logically
fit into your degree program
Advanced Certificate Transfer Credit
Up to 3 graduate-level credits can be transferred
Credits with a letter grade of B or better are eligible
Credits must be less than six years old at the time of admission and must logically
fit into your degree program
Community College Students: A Pathway to Your Bachelor's Degree
You can transfer up to 79 credits from an associate degree through the Pathways Transfer
Program (PTP).
The Pathways Program is designed for students who have (or are about to receive) an
associate degree from a community college and now want to pursue a bachelor’s degree
with SUNY Empire.
See the full list of our community college partners here: Pathways Transfer Program
