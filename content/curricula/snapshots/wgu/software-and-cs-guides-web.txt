L108@P3: your	new	terms	while	you	are	at	WGU.	Each	term	is	six	months	long.	Longer	terms	and	continuous

L110@P3: you	would	experience	at	a	more	traditional	university.	At	the	end	of	every	six-month	term,	you	and	your
L111@P3: program	mentor	will	review	the	progress	you	have	made	and	revise	your	Degree	Plan	for	your	next	sixmonth	term.

L113@P3: every	term.	We	call	this	“On-Time	Progress,”	denoting	that	you	are	on	track	and	making	progress	toward
L114@P3: on-time	graduation.	As	full-time	students,	graduate	students	must	enroll	in	at	least	8	competency	units
L115@P3: each	term,	and	undergraduate	students	must	enroll	in	at	least	12	competency	units	each	term.	Completing
L116@P3: at	least	these	minimum	enrollments	is	essential	to	On-Time	Progress	and	serves	as	a	baseline	from	which
L117@P3: you	may	accelerate	your	program.	We	measure	your	progress	based	onthe	courses	you	are	able	to	pass,

L119@P3: demonstrating	that	you	have	mastered	skills	and	knowledge	in	your	degree	program.	For	comparison	to
L120@P3: traditional	grading	systems,	passing	a	course	means	you	have	demonstrated	competency	equivalent	to	a
L121@P3: “B”	grade	or	better.
