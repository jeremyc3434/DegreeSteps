L238: #### How can I use StraighterLine to earn credits toward my degree at the School of Business at WGU?
L239: StraighterLine gives you a flexible, affordable option for online college credit. As a StraighterLine partner school, the School of Business at WGU has approved select courses for transfer credit. Those courses are specified above under “Course Equivalency.” To earn credit, all you have to do is enroll in the StraighterLine courses that align with your degree program and pass with a grade of 70% or better. Once you're done, it’s as simple as requesting a transcript to be sent to your school.

L252: ## About the School of Business at WGU
L253: 
L254: 【52†wgu.edu†www.wgu.edu】
L255: 
L256: The School of Business at Western Governors University (WGU) is part of WGU’s fully online, nonprofit university. It was created in 1997 to expand access to affordable, high-quality education.
L257: The School of Business offers programs designed for working adults who want to advance or start careers in fields such as accounting, finance, management, marketing, and business analytics. Like all of WGU, it uses a competency-based model, allowing students to progress as soon as they demonstrate mastery of the material rather than waiting for traditional semester schedules.
L258: With flat-rate tuition per six-month term, the School of Business provides a flexible, career-focused pathway for motivated learners pursuing undergraduate and graduate degrees in business and related disciplines.
