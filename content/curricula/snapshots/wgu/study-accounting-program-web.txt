## B.S. in Accounting Degree Program Overview

WGU's online accounting program consists of 40 courses. There are four different areas of study in the B.S. in Accounting program: general education, business management, business core, and accounting.

### General Education

To meet the general education requirements, students will learn about topics like communication skills (written and spoken), social sciences and humanities, foundational mathematics, and more. Learning these topics alongside accounting-focused subjects produces more well-rounded individuals and professionals in the future. These courses can be a great starting point for students looking to transfer credits and reduce the time it takes to finish their degree.

### Business Management

The business management subject area sharpens a student's ability to manage different business elements like projects, organizational changes, and operations and supply chains. These courses seek to improve students' understanding of effective business leadership through discussions of organizational behavior, communication, business ethics, and leadership.

### Business Core

The business core subject area teaches the fundamentals of business through courses like Fundamentals for Success in Business, Fundamentals of Spreadsheets and Data Presentations, and Principles of Economics. Students will discover what it takes to run a business efficiently by learning how to manage a global business, apply managerial financial skills, think strategically and innovatively, and more.

After taking the mentioned courses in business, students then apply their knowledge through the Business Simulation course. This course allows students to demonstrate mastery through the application of their skills in a simulated business setting.

### Accounting

During this program, students will take Intermediate Accounting I, II, and III. These classes cover accounting topics such as compliance, acquisitions, and depreciation. Financial Accounting will cover how accounting principles apply in business settings. Cost and Managerial Accounting will discuss how to use and interpret accounting data for managerial decisions.

Classes like Taxation I and Business Law for Accountants teach the legal complexities of taxation for businesses and individuals along with common legal issues involved in this career. Through the Accounting Information Systems (AIS) course, students can remain up-to-date on industry standards for the daily work accountants do. With AIS, students will become knowledgeable about a variety of management and financial reporting systems.

To round out this subject area, students will learn how to go through the entire auditing process via the Auditing class.

## Western Governors University Admissions Requirements

Students interested in a B.S. in Accounting degree through WGU must first enroll. There are two types of admissions requirements for students: general admissions requirements and course-specific requirements.

### General Admissions Requirements

Rolling admissions at WGU means that students can enroll at any time. However, the start date for classes would be on the first day of the month after the completion of enrollment.

Here is a step-by-step list of how to enroll at WGU:

  1. Complete the admission form.
  2. Submit any official transcripts to WGU to identify transfer credits that can go toward your degree. The deadline for this is usually on the 5th of the month before your ideal start date. The information can be submitted through WGU's free transcript service or independently.
  3. If you would like financial aid for your tuition fees, then filling out the FAFSA is required.
  4. Complete and submit your Commit to Start documents.

### WGU B.S. in Accounting Admissions Requirements

There are admissions requirements specific to the accounting program in addition to the general admissions requirements. To enroll in WGU's B.S. in Accounting degree program, students must possess a high school diploma or an equivalent and one of the following options:

  * Transcripts showing the completion of college-level coursework with a GPA of at least 2.0
  * A bachelor's or associate's degree from an accredited post-secondary educational institution
  * High school transcripts with a GPA of at least 2.0

Satisfying these requirements will show how prepared you are to successfully complete the B.S. in Accounting coursework.

## FAQ

Do Study.com course equivalencies change over time?

Yes, and this matters more than many students realize. WGU periodically updates its transfer articulations as degree programs evolve, as ACE or NCCRS recommendations are revised, or as WGU adds and removes courses from its accepted transfer list. WGU conducted a notable articulation update in early 2026 affecting multiple partner providers. A course that transferred successfully for a student a year ago may no longer map to the same requirement. Always verify in the Partners Portal before starting any course.

Can I complete Study.com courses after I start at WGU?

No. WGU does not award transfer credit after a student has officially begun their program. The cutoff is your term start date, not your application date. If you complete a Study.com course after your first WGU term begins, it will not be applied toward your degree. Plan accordingly and complete all transfer coursework before you enroll.

## FAQ

Can I transfer more than 90 credits to WGU from Study.com?

No. WGU's 90-credit combined cap is a firm limit for undergraduate programs. Credits beyond 90 hours cannot be applied toward your degree from any source, including Study.com. At least 25% of your degree must be completed at WGU.

Does WGU's transfer credit limit change by degree program?

The 75% rule is consistent across undergraduate programs, but the total credit hours vary by program length. A 120-credit program allows up to 90 transfer credits. Programs with more or fewer total credits will have a proportionally different ceiling.
