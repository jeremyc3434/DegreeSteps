## Computer Science Program Overview

In the WGU B.S. in Computer Science degree program, students must complete 26 courses in the computer science curriculum and 11 general education courses. General education coursework covers topics in probability and statistics, discrete mathematics, calculus, and systems thinking and applications. Students must also complete requirements in health and wellness, communication, composition, and American politics. A natural science lab is required under the general education curriculum as well. Transfer credits earned online can satisfy many of these general education requirements.

The computer science portion of the program contains classes in areas of web development, data management, software, full stack engineering, programming, and information technology management. In these classes, students can expect to learn about fundamental programming languages, Java fundamentals and advanced concepts, data modeling, software engineering processes, and Linux foundations. Other required courses cover network security, business IT applications, and information security.

The program's ten courses dedicated most specifically to computer science concepts cover computer architecture, data structures, introductory and advanced AI, coding, object-oriented design, memory management, file systems, and AI optimization. This part of the program also contains a team-oriented project development assignment where students submit a technical report discussing a data product, and it includes a business proposal describing project implementation.

### WGU General Admissions

While prospective students can apply to WGU at any time during the year, new students begin classes on the first of every month. First, candidates must complete and submit an admissions form. Official transcripts (either high school or completed college) must also be submitted. Students who will be using financial aid to help pay tuition must send in a completed FAFSA form. After all of these materials have been submitted to WGU, students then submit Commit to Start documents via an online process. Included in these documents are a verification of the start date, confirmation of enrollment in the B.S. in Computer Science program, and an agreement to enrollment terms and conditions.

### WGU Undergraduate Requirements

Aside from the general admissions requirements described above, students seeking to enter the computer science bachelor's degree program must meet specific program requirements in order to enroll. The first requirement is that they must possess a high school diploma or equivalent.

Having met the first requirement, students then display readiness in entering the B.S. in Computer Science program through meeting one of two options. The first option is that they submit a transcript of college-level courses showing a minimum cumulative GPA of 2.75 and completion (with a grade of at least a C) of a precalculus or higher-level class from a provider approved by WGU. In the second option, students demonstrate a high school GPA of 3.0 or higher and at least a B grade in an honors, IB, or AP math class.

## WGU B.S. in Computer Science FAQs

### Is the online computer science degree accredited?

Yes, Western Governors University has earned multiple accreditations, most notably from the Northwest Commission on Colleges and Universities (NWCCU), the highest form of accreditation. In addition, the B.S. in Computer Science program is accredited by ABET, a leading global accreditor of computer technology programs.

### How quickly can I complete my online computer science bachelor's degree?

Since the B.S. in Computer Science program at WGU is self-paced, completion times vary. However, by maximizing transfer credit from Study.com and your other sources, and with commitment and dedication, you can complete your degree in less time. The exact time required will depend on the number of credits you are able to transfer and how many classes you take each semester. On average, WGU students complete online degree programs in 2.5 years.

### How hard is the computer science bachelor's degree program?

The computer science program at WGU, while challenging, concentrates on one competency at a time, a model that allows you to focus on mastering a single topic before moving forward. This plan, along with the flexibility of online studies that allows for study time when it best suits a student's schedule and readiness to focus, makes learning more effective and stress-free.

### How many credits are there in the bachelor's degree program in computer science?

Rather than traditional credits based on hours, WGU uses competency units (CUs) that measure one's mastery of the subject. To earn the B.S. degree in computer science, students must complete 117 CUs.

### Is a bachelor's degree in computer science worth earning?

In most cases, a bachelor's degree is required for many positions in the computer science field, so it is definitely worth earning this degree. By obtaining the degree through WGU and Study.com transfer credits, you can save time and money so you can advance your career for less.

## FAQ

Do Study.com course equivalencies change over time?

Yes, and this matters more than many students realize. WGU periodically updates its transfer articulations as degree programs evolve, as ACE or NCCRS recommendations are revised, or as WGU adds and removes courses from its accepted transfer list. WGU conducted a notable articulation update in early 2026 affecting multiple partner providers. A course that transferred successfully for a student a year ago may no longer map to the same requirement. Always verify in the Partners Portal before starting any course.

Can I complete Study.com courses after I start at WGU?

No. WGU does not award transfer credit after a student has officially begun their program. The cutoff is your term start date, not your application date. If you complete a Study.com course after your first WGU term begins, it will not be applied toward your degree. Plan accordingly and complete all transfer coursework before you enroll.
