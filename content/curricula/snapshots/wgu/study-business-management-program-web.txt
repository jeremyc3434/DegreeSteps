## WGU Admissions Requirements

WGU's business management bachelor's degree program is offered through the School of Business. Students must satisfy Western Governor's general admission requirements and School of Business undergraduate degree requirements.

### WGU General Admissions

New student cohorts start on the first of every month, and students can apply to Western Governors University at any time. The admissions process starts with filling out the admission form. Next, submit your official transcripts. Make sure you've earned all the transferable credit you plan to apply to your degree before enrolling! ### WGU School of Business Undergraduate Requirements

To apply to enroll in a bachelor's degree in business program students must meet all general admissions requirements. Additionally, they must have earned a high school diploma or equivalent like the GED.

Students must then satisfy one of three options. Option 1 is to have completed college level coursework with a minimum GPA of 2.0. ## Business Management Program Overview

The business management program is divided into a few components. General education courses are required for all bachelor's degree courses and expose students to an array of topics in math, science, English, social science, and more. General education is an excellent area to get started when you are earning transfer credit online. In fact, if students have some previous college credit they most likely already have credit for some of the general education requirements.

Business degree students take general business courses, called the Business Core. These courses are introductory courses for various domains of business. In the Business Core students take courses in finance, accounting, economics, and more. Students won't go deep in any of these areas but will get a good overview to be an effective business leader. Students take Principles of Management in the Business Core and then will go deeper into management topics in the Business Management area of the degree. There are nine courses in leadership, business communication, business ethics, operations and supply chain management, change management, organizational behavior, project management and more. This coursework prepares students for a variety of business roles. Students can earn transfer credit for several of the Business Core and Business Management courses, including for Principles of Management, Principles of Economics, and Organizational Behavior.

The program also includes five courses in Human Resources, which is important for managers to understand. Students take courses in talent acquisition, labor law, training and development, and rewards. There is are additional courses in Sales Management and IT.

The final step the degree program is the Business Management Capstone Written Project. In this project students utilize the competencies they mastered throughout their degree program to write a comprehensive business plan. The capstone is challenging, but students who have completed all the program courses through transfer credit and at WGU are well-prepared to create a well-structured, thorough business plan.
