## Frequently Asked Questions

Is WGU's cybersecurity degree accredited?

Yes. WGU's B.S. Cybersecurity and Information Assurance is ABET-accredited, WGU holds the National Center of Academic Excellence in Cyber Defense designation from the NSA, and Western Governors University itself is institutionally accredited by the Northwest Commission on Colleges and Universities, the regional accreditor for its home state of Utah.

How long does WGU's cybersecurity degree take?

WGU reports that 60% of graduates finish within 29 months. Terms are six months long, and full-time students take at least 12 competency units per term, so every unit you transfer in from Study.com is one you don't complete at WGU.

How many credits is WGU's cybersecurity degree?

The degree is 122 competency units across 41 courses, WGU's equivalent of credit hours. A competency unit is equivalent to one semester credit, so the degree is comparable to a standard 120-credit bachelor's program. Study.com's map currently matches 47 units that can be transferred in before you enroll.

How many Study.com credits transfer into WGU's cybersecurity degree?

Study.com's current map matches 15 courses worth 47 competency units: 9 general education courses and 6 core IT and cybersecurity foundation courses. Upper-level cybersecurity courses such as Penetration Testing and Managing Cloud Security, and the capstone, are completed at WGU.

Can I transfer credits after I start at WGU?

No. WGU does not award transfer credit after a student begins the program, and official transcripts must arrive by the 5th of the month before your intended start date. Finish your Study.com courses and request your transcript well before that deadline.

Should I earn CompTIA certifications before enrolling or through WGU?

Both routes count. WGU accepts certifications earned within the last five years as transfer credit, and the degree includes exam vouchers for several CompTIA exams. Ask a WGU enrollment counselor how a certification would apply before paying for an exam.

How much does WGU's cybersecurity degree cost?

WGU charges $4,425 tuition plus a $200 e-books and resources fee per six-month term, a flat rate that also holds for terms starting October 1, 2026. Tuition doesn't change based on how many courses you finish in the term, only how many terms you complete.

## Cybersecurity and Information Assurance Program Overview

A total of 24 courses in WGU's Cybersecurity and Information Assurance program are dedicated to concepts in cloud security, hacking countermeasures, information assurance, network security, risk management, and cyber law. The remaining 10 courses are general education requirements covering health and wellness, composition, systems thinking, communication, algebra, American politics, and probability and statistics. Transfer credits from outside sources can satisfy many of the general education curriculum requirements.

For this online bachelor's degree program in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance, students are expected to master such concepts as:

  * Cryptography
  * Threat identification and solution methods for networking technologies
  * Data modeling and database modification
  * Information security management
  * Risk analysis and data recovery
  * Basics of Linux Essentials, CompTIA Network+, SQL, and Python
  * Information integrity assurance and data protection
  * Firewalls, encryption, and incident handling
  * Penetration testing and vulnerability analysis methods

Students also have the opportunity to learn about information security ethics, laws and regulations in cybersecurity, scripting, and IT project management. The program culminates in a capstone project, wherein students apply knowledge of concepts learned throughout their studies to develop a technical proposal and implement the work outlined in that proposal. They must also submit a report detailing the results of this implementation.

### WGU General Admissions

Applying to WGU is an easy process that starts with the submission of an admission form. Classes for new students begin on the first of each month.

Official transcripts from high school or college are required, along with the admission form. Transcripts can be sent independently or using WGU's free service. Students intending to use financial aid to assist in paying tuition are required to submit a FASFA form.

As a final step, students must complete the online Commit to Start process. This involves submitting documents, including an agreement to enrollment terms and conditions, verification of your start date, and confirmation of your enrollment in the B.S. in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance program.

### WGU Undergraduate Requirements

Specific program requirements for the online Bachelor's in Cybersecurity and Information Assurance must be met in addition to completing the general admissions process. First, students must have a high school diploma or equivalent. Then, they must demonstrate readiness for entering this bachelor's degree program by satisfying one of the following five options:

  * Submit college transcripts with a cumulative GPA of at least 2.75
  * Hold a bachelor's or associate's degree from an accredited institution
  * Submit verification of a transferable IT certification
  * Submit high school transcripts showing a cumulative GPA of at least 3.0
  * Submit transcripts of completed 300-level or higher IT coursework
