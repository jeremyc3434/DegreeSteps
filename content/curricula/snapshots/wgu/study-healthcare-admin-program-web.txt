### WGU General Admissions

WGU's general admissions process is easy. The first step is submitting an admissions form, along with an official high school or completed college transcript. Transcripts can be submitted independently or through WGU's free transcript service. A FAFSA form must also accompany these documents for students intending to use federal aid to pay their tuition. Once all of these documents are reviewed, students then pass through the Commit to Start process. This involves submitting online documents that confirm the student's start date, verify their entry into the healthcare administration program, and agree to WGU's enrollment terms and conditions. Students can apply to WGU at any time during the year. Classes for new students start the first day of each month.

### WGU Undergraduate Requirements

Each of WGU's programs has separate admissions requirements in addition to those in general admissions.

For the B.S. in Healthcare Administration program, students first must possess a high school diploma or equivalent. Then, they must satisfy one of the following three options to show their readiness to enter this particular program:

  * Option 1: Submit college-level transcripts showing a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0
  * Option 2: Hold a bachelor's or associate's degree from an accredited institution
  * Option 3: Submit high school transcripts with a cumulative GPA of at least 2.0

## FAQ

Do Study.com course equivalencies change over time?

Yes, and this matters more than many students realize. WGU periodically updates its transfer articulations as degree programs evolve, as ACE or NCCRS recommendations are revised, or as WGU adds and removes courses from its accepted transfer list. WGU conducted a notable articulation update in early 2026 affecting multiple partner providers. A course that transferred successfully for a student a year ago may no longer map to the same requirement. Always verify in the Partners Portal before starting any course.

Can I complete Study.com courses after I start at WGU?

No. WGU does not award transfer credit after a student has officially begun their program. The cutoff is your term start date, not your application date. If you complete a Study.com course after your first WGU term begins, it will not be applied toward your degree. Plan accordingly and complete all transfer coursework before you enroll.

Does Study.com require proctored exams for credits that transfer to WGU?

All Study.com courses use open-book, non-proctored final exams. Unlike some alternative credit providers, proctoring is generally not required to earn an ACE- or NCCRS-recommended transcript from Study.com.

Can Study.com credits satisfy WGU's general education requirements across multiple degree programs?

Yes, for many programs. Subjects like English composition, college algebra or statistics, introductory psychology, and social sciences are among the most consistently accepted Study.com courses across WGU undergraduate programs. The same general education course may satisfy the requirement in a business degree, an IT degree, and a healthcare administration degree simultaneously. However, it is recommended to always verify your specific program's requirements in the Partners Portal, as some programs specify particular course distributions.

What happens if a Study.com course I completed is no longer accepted by WGU by the time I submit my transcript?

If WGU removes a course from its equivalency list between the time you completed it and when your transcript is evaluated, WGU may apply it only as a general elective or decline to apply it at all. That is why it's important to stay apprised of the current transfer credit guidelines.

# What Is WGU's Transfer Credit Policy for Study.com?

Last Updated: March 2026 | By Study.com Editorial Staff

## Key Takeaways

  * WGU accepts up to 75% of total degree requirements via transfer credit, approximately 90 credits in a standard 120-credit bachelor's program.
  * The 90-credit cap is a combined limit that applies to all transfer sources together: Study.com, community college credits, CLEP exams, military training, and other alternative credit providers.
  * Study.com credits are accepted under WGU's alternative credit framework when transcripted through ACE or NCCRS.
  * At least 25% of your WGU degree must be completed directly at WGU, regardless of how many transfer credits you hold.
  * WGU evaluates credits course-by-course against specific program competencies, not just credit hours.

## FAQ

Can I transfer more than 90 credits to WGU from Study.com?

No. WGU's 90-credit combined cap is a firm limit for undergraduate programs. Credits beyond 90 hours cannot be applied toward your degree from any source, including Study.com. At least 25% of your degree must be completed at WGU.

Does WGU's transfer credit limit change by degree program?

The 75% rule is consistent across undergraduate programs, but the total credit hours vary by program length. A 120-credit program allows up to 90 transfer credits. Programs with more or fewer total credits will have a proportionally different ceiling. For example, if you are pursuing WGU's B.S. in Human Resource Management, transferable human resources courses count toward that program's cap. Confirm your specific program's cap with a WGU enrollment counselor.

Is there a separate or lower cap specifically for Study.com credits?

No. Study.com credits count toward the same combined 90-credit cap as all other transfer sources. WGU does not apply a separate or lower sub-limit to ACE- or NCCRS-recommended credits.

Can I start Study.com courses before applying to WGU?

You can, but you will get significantly better results by applying first. WGU's initial transfer evaluation and the Partners Portal will show you exactly which requirements remain open and which Study.com courses map to them. Starting without this information increases the risk of completing courses that do not transfer as intended.

What happens if a course mapping changes after I complete a Study.com course?

Policy changes can affect which courses WGU accepts, even after you have completed them but before your transcript is evaluated. We recommend submitting transcripts promptly after finishing each course and to stay in active contact with your WGU enrollment counselor throughout your pre-enrollment period. If a course no longer maps to the intended competency, ask your counselor whether it can count as a general elective or what an approved alternative would be.

Does completing Study.com courses affect my WGU GPA?

No. Transfer credits are applied to your degree plan but are not factored into WGU's academic record. WGU uses a competency-based, mastery model, so academic progress is measured through WGU's own assessments, not by GPA.
