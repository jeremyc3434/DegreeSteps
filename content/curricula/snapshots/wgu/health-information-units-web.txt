L122@P3: Continuous Enrollment, On Time Progress, and Satisfactory Academic
L123@P3: Progress
L124@P3: WGU is a “continuous enrollment” institution, which means you will be automatically enrolled in each of
L125@P3: your new terms while you are at WGU. Each term is six months long. Longer terms and continuous
L126@P3: enrollment allow you to focus on your studies without the hassle of unnatural breaks between terms that
L127@P3: you would experience at a more traditional university. At the end of every six-month term, you and your
L128@P3: program mentor will review the progress you have made and revise your Degree Plan for your next sixmonth term.
L129@P3: WGU requires that students make measurable progress toward the completion of their degree programs
L130@P3: every term. We call this “On-Time Progress,” denoting that you are on track and making progress toward
L131@P3: on-time graduation. As full-time students, graduate students must enroll in at least 8 competency units
L132@P3: each term, and undergraduate students must enroll in at least 12 competency units each term. Completing
L133@P3: at least these minimum enrollments is essential to On-Time Progress and serves as a baseline from which
L134@P3: you may accelerate your program. We measure your progress based onthe courses you are able to pass,
L135@P3: not on your accumulation of credit hours or course grades. Every time you pass a course, you are
L136@P3: demonstrating that you have mastered skills and knowledge in your degree program. For comparison to
L137@P3: traditional grading systems, passing a course means you have demonstrated competency equivalent to a
L138@P3: “B” grade or better.
L139@P3: WGU assigns competency units to each course in order to track your progress through the program. A
L140@P3: competency unit is equivalent to one semester credit of learning. Some courses may be assigned 3
L141@P3: competency units while others may be as large as 12 competency units.
L142@P3: Satisfactory Academic Progress (SAP) is particularly important to students on financial aid because you
L143@P3: must achieve SAP in order to maintain eligibility for financial aid. We will measure your SAP quantitatively
L144@P3: by reviewing the number of competency units you have completed each term. In order to remain in good
L145@P3-4: http://cm.wgu.edu/academic standing, you must complete at least 66.67% of the units you attempt over the length of your
L146@P4: program—including any courses you add to your term to accelerate your progress. Additionally, during
L147@P4: your first term at WGU you must pass at least 3 competency units in order to remain eligible for financial
L148@P4: aid. We know that SAP is complex, so please contact a financial aid counselor should you have additional
L149@P4: questions.
