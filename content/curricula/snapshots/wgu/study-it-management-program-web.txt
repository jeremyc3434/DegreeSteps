## How Do You Apply to WGU?

  1. Submit WGU's admission form: Classes for new students begin on the first of every month.
  2. Send official transcripts. WGU offers a free transcript-request service for high school or college records, or you can send them yourself.
  3. Complete the FAFSA if you plan to use financial aid: Submitting early gives WGU's financial aid office time to determine your aid package before your term starts.
  4. Send your Study.com transcript: Study.com sends it within two business days so it arrives by the 5th of the month before your start date, per WGU's transfer policy. WGU does not award transfer credit after you begin. See WGU's 2026 transfer credit policy changes for what's changed recently.
  5. Complete the online Commit to Start process: Agree to enrollment terms, confirm your start date, and confirm enrollment in the B.S. Information Technology Management program.
