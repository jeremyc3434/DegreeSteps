FAQ
Common Questions Answered.
Everything you need to know about UPI Study credits, pricing, and how they transfer to universities worldwide.
Can I earn college credit without enrolling in a full degree program?
Yes. UPI Study allows students to take individual courses and earn ACE and NCCRS-recommended college credit completely independently — no university enrollment, no application, no admission process required. Complete the courses, request your official transcript ($20), then submit it to the university of your choice.
How do I check if my university will accept UPI Study credits?
Two ways: 1) Check whether your university is listed in our Find my College directory — if it is, you're good. 2) Check your university's credit transfer policy. If it says they accept credit recommended by ACE or NCCRS ("ACE/NCCRS-evaluated" sources), UPI Study courses can be considered. ACE and NCCRS have over 2,100 cooperating colleges that will consider accepting credits; the final decision is with the universities. When in doubt, book a call with our team.
What's the difference between ACE and NCCRS credit recommendations?
ACE (American Council on Education) evaluates non-traditional learning and recommends credit to colleges and universities. NCCRS (National College Credit Recommendation Service), operated by the New York State Education Department, does the same. Both are nationally recognized standards. UPI Study courses carry recommendations from both bodies — the same evaluation system used by CLEP, DSST, and military training programs.
Can military students use UPI Study?
Yes — military students are some of our most active members. UPI Study credits use the same ACE and NCCRS standards as military training evaluations, so they can be stacked with existing military training credits on a single Excelsior OneTranscript. Our subscription plans, from $99/month, are for US citizens, permanent residents and military — and for students of our partner universities, wherever they live. There are no additional fees for course materials, textbooks, or resources — they are included in your membership. The official transcript is $20.
Do UPI Study credits ever expire?
No. ACE and NCCRS credit recommendations do not expire. Earn credits now and use them years later for a university application, promotion eligibility, or degree completion — whenever you're ready.
Does UPI Study offer single course options?
Yes — international students can purchase individual courses from our full course catalog for $250 per course (3–4 college credits each). Multi-course bundles of 6, 12, or 24 courses are also available at discounted rates. US citizens, permanent residents, military and partner-university students can access all 90+ courses through a subscription plan.
How does UPI Study compare to taking courses at a community college?
Community college is a great option but typically costs $300–$600 per credit hour, requires fixed class schedules, and involves semester deadlines. UPI Study offers ACE & NCCRS-recommended credits from $41/month (US) or $250/course (international) with no deadlines, no fixed class times, and no campus required. ACE and NCCRS have over 2,100 cooperating colleges that will consider accepting credits; the final decision is with the universities.
Can online college credit from UPI Study help reduce the cost of a degree?
Yes — this is one of the main reasons students choose UPI Study. Many universities accept up to 90 transfer credits; the final decision is with each university. By earning transferable credits before enrolling, students can skip the first two-three years of tuition, enter as Juniors/Seniors, and dramatically cut the total cost of their degree. Some students save $40,000–$80,000 in tuition by building credits this way.
Is UPI Study a good option for adult learners returning to school?
It's one of the best options available. UPI Study is fully self-paced with no deadlines — adult learners with jobs, families, and unpredictable schedules can study whenever they have time, pause when they don't, and pick back up without penalty. Most of our adult learners complete 6–12 courses in their first year around their existing lives.
Who should use UPI Study?
UPI Study is a strong fit for: students wanting to reduce degree cost through credit transfer, first responders (police, fire, EMS) needing credits for promotion, military members building toward a degree, adult learners returning to education, international students seeking affordable US-recognized credits, and anyone who wants flexibility that a traditional classroom can't offer.
How many credits can I earn through UPI Study?
Many universities accept up to 90 transfer credits; the final decision is with each university. UPI Study's 90+ courses each carry 3–4 ACE and NCCRS-recommended credits, and you can combine them with other ACE/NCCRS sources like CLEP exams, DSST exams, or military training.
What is the Excelsior University OneTranscript?
The Excelsior OneTranscript consolidates credits from multiple ACE and NCCRS sources — CLEP, DSST, UPI Study, military training — into a single official academic document. It's one of the most widely accepted transcripts for non-traditional learners. Submit it to any university for credit evaluation. UPI Study credits are fully compatible.
How long does it take to complete a UPI Study course?
There is no fixed length. Every course is self-paced with no deadlines, so it takes as long as you need: you can move faster through subjects you already know, or slow down during busy periods at work or home — without any penalty.
What is the 1,000-student enrollment cap?
UPI Study caps US subscription enrollment at 1,000 active members to ensure every student receives personalized advisor support and a high-quality experience. This is not a marketing tactic — it's a quality-control decision. If you're considering enrolling, we recommend not waiting.
What happens if I need to pause or cancel my subscription?
You can pause or cancel your subscription at any time with no penalty and no questions asked. Credits you've already earned remain valid permanently. If you pause and return later, you pick up exactly where you left off.
