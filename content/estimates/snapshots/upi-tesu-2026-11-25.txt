FAQ
Common Questions Answered.
Everything you need to know about UPI Study credits, pricing, and how they transfer to Thomas Edison State University. The TESU-specific answers come from TESU's equivalency list for UPI Study and TESU's 2026-27 catalog.
Does Thomas Edison State University officially accept UPI Study credits?
Yes. Thomas Edison State University lists UPI Study as an approved educational provider, and TESU's own list for UPI Study courses (May 2026, updated October 2026) gives the TESU course for every row marked Verified by TESU. TESU makes the final evaluation on every transcript. You can view the official agreement here.
How does Physics I transfer to TESU?
PHY-1110 Physics I (3 credits at TESU), on TESU's own list for UPI Study courses (May 2026, updated October 2026).
Which TESU course is UPI Study's Introduction to Psychology?
PSY-1010 Introduction to Psychology (3 credits at TESU), on TESU's own list for UPI Study courses (May 2026, updated October 2026).
What does English Composition I become at TESU?
TESU has not listed it yet, so UPI Study recommends ENC-1010 Writing for Success (3 credits at TESU); TESU confirms it when it evaluates your transcript. TESU's first-year writing course.
Which courses on the TESU chart are recommended by UPI Study?
Business Essentials (BUS-1010 Introduction to Business), Foundations of Leadership (LDR-3050 Foundations of Leadership), Project Management (MAN-4350 Project Management), Business Math (BUS-2100 Quantitative Skills for Business), College Algebra (MAT-1210 College Algebra), Precalculus (MAT-1290 Precalculus), among others. TESU has not listed these yet, so the chart shows the closest TESU course or area UPI Study recommends; TESU confirms each one when it evaluates your transcript.
What do “Verified by TESU” and “Recommended by UPI Study” mean in the chart?
Verified by TESU means TESU itself gave the equivalency: the course is on TESU's list for UPI Study courses (May 2026, updated October 2026), with the TESU course it becomes. Recommended by UPI Study means TESU has not listed it yet, so our team picked the closest course in TESU's 2026-27 catalog and linked it; TESU confirms it when it evaluates your transcript.

Current Trends in Computer Science and IT (COS-1999), Computer Concepts and Applications (CIS-1070), Introduction to HTML and CSS (CAP-2800), Introduction to JavaScript (CAP-2530), Introduction to Networking (ITS-1400), Fundamentals of Information Technology (TEC-2610), Introduction to Linux (ITS-2610), Introduction to Java (COS-2080), Programming in Python (COS-2050), Introduction to Computing (CAP-1700), Programming in C (COS-1160), Programming in C++ (COS-2130), Data Structures and Algorithms (COS-2410), Network and Systems Security (CYB-1240), Cybersecurity (CYB-1200), Database Fundamentals (ITS-1300), Software Engineering (CIS-2510), Introduction to Operating Systems (COS-2400), Introduction to Artificial Intelligence (COS-4510), Database Programming (ITS-2310), System Analysis and Design (CIS-3200) and Discrete Mathematics (COS-2610). Ethics in Technology is recommended by UPI Study as ETH-2300 Ethics in the Digital Age.
Which UPI Study business courses has TESU verified?

Business Communication, Business Essentials, Foundations of Leadership, Project Management and Business Math are recommended by UPI Study; the chart shows the TESU course for each (Business Communication as COM-2020 Introduction to Business Communications, which TESU decides how to apply).
I'm in a TESU health or science program. What can I transfer?
Verified by TESU: Intro to Biology 1 and 2 (BIO-1110 and BIO-1120), Chemistry I (CHE-1110), Chemistry Lab (CHE-1280), Physics I (PHY-1110), Environmental Science (ENS-2000), Healthcare Finance and Budgeting (HCM-4050), Healthcare Organization and Management (HCM-3070), Human Resource Management in Healthcare (HCM-4060) and Introduction to Psychology (PSY-1010). Recommended by UPI Study: Medical Terminology (APS-1000), Anatomy & Physiology I and II with their labs (TESU's BIO-2110 and BIO-2120 include the lab) and Physics Lab (PHY-1280). Which UPI Study math, writing and humanities courses does TESU take?
Verified by TESU: Calculus 1 and 2 (MAT-2310 and MAT-2320), Principles of Statistics (ECO-2700), Quantitative Analysis (BUS-2100), Discrete Mathematics (COS-2610), Advanced Technical Writing (ENG-2010), Introduction to Sociology (SOC-1010) and Principles of Philosophy (PHI-1010). Recommended by UPI Study: College Algebra (MAT-1210), Precalculus (MAT-1290), Calculus 3 (MAT-3310), English Composition I and II (ENC-1010 and ENC-1020), American Literature (LIT-2050), Art History I and II (ART-1000 and HUM-1040), U.S. History I and II (HIS-1130 and HIS-1140), Critical Thinking & Analysis (PHI-1300) and Ethics in the Social Sciences (SOS-4500). TESU has no French course, so French I and II are recommended as humanities elective credit. Where a course counts in your degree (general education, major or elective) depends on your TESU program.
Does TESU accept Discrete Mathematics?
Yes. TESU's Dean reviewed the course and approved it in transfer as COS-2610 Discrete Structures, for all students from now on. If you have a concern about how it applies to your degree, take it to your TESU Academic Advisor.
How much does TESU charge per credit, and how does UPI Study compare?
TESU's per-credit tuition is $449 for New Jersey residents (including Pennsylvania) and $584 for out-of-state students (its published rates, checked 13 October 2026), so one 3-credit course is $1,347 to $1,752 in tuition. At UPI Study the monthly plan is $89 for every course, the 4-month plan is $279, and Lifetime access is a one-time payment. Select courses in the chart above to see your own estimate.
Is there a discount for TESU students?
Partner pricing for every TESU student, US or international: $89 a month on the monthly plan, and 25% off your first purchase with a current student's ReferOn code. Athena, the chat on this page, can tell you which plan is cheapest for the courses you need.
Can TESU reject a UPI Study course?
TESU makes the final evaluation on every transcript. Courses marked Verified by TESU are on TESU's own list for UPI Study; courses marked Recommended by UPI Study are evaluated from their ACE or NCCRS recommendation. The usual reasons a course does not count: you already have credit for that TESU course, or the course sits outside your degree plan. The note under the row tells you what to ask for.
Will UPI Study appear on my TESU transcript?
TESU records the credit as transfer credit under the TESU course it was evaluated as, or as elective credit. The UPI Study transcript you send is the official record of the course; it carries the ACE or NCCRS credit recommendation, which is how TESU recognises it.
I have both Calculus 1 and Calculus 2. What do I get at TESU?
Both are verified by TESU: Calculus 1 becomes MAT-2310 Calculus I and Calculus 2 becomes MAT-2320 Calculus II. For Calculus 3, UPI Study recommends TESU's MAT-3310 Calculus III.
What about Anatomy & Physiology I and II and the lab courses?
TESU's Anatomy and Physiology courses include the lab and carry 4 credits: BIO-2110 (I) and BIO-2120 (II). UPI Study recommends taking Anatomy & Physiology I with its lab and asking TESU for BIO-2110, and the same for II and BIO-2120. On its own, each Anatomy & Physiology lab is 2 elective credits. Chemistry Lab is verified by TESU as CHE-1280 General Chemistry I Lab (1 credit).
Can international TESU students use UPI Study?
Yes. TESU students anywhere get the same partner pricing. UPI Study courses are online and self-paced, and the transcript is sent electronically to TESU.
How long will a UPI Study course take before I can send it to TESU?
As long as you choose. UPI Study courses are self-paced: you can finish as fast or as slow as you want. There is no minimum or maximum time to finish a course; plan around TESU's own dates, such as your application or term start, and leave about two days after your last submission for grading.
Can I take UPI Study courses now and send them to TESU in a few years?
Yes. Credit you earn stays valid for 10 years, even if a course's ACE (3-year) or NCCRS (5-year) listing ends in the meantime, and UPI Study renews its listings as they end.
How does my UPI Study credit get to TESU?
UPI Study sends it for you. When the course shows 100% in your dashboard, request your official transcript ($20, or $25 once for unlimited requests), enter TESU's transcript address, and UPI Study emails it from its registrar; you get a confirmation email (check spam too).

About the University
About Thomas Edison State University
Founded in 1972 and based in Trenton, New Jersey, Thomas Edison State University (TESU) is a public university and one of New Jersey's 11 senior public institutions of higher education. TESU was established specifically to serve adult learners, and remains a national leader in flexible, online higher education. Accredited by the Middle States Commission on Higher Education (MSCHE), TESU offers more than 100 associate, bachelor's, master's, and doctoral degree programs across five academic schools. The university has pioneered credit for prior learning for over 50 years — accepting ACE and NCCRS-evaluated credits, military training, CLEP and DSST exams, and experiential learning portfolios.
Transfer Credit Information
TESU has one of the most generous transfer credit policies in the country. The university accepts ACE and NCCRS-recommended credits, including UPI Study coursework, as transfer credit. Students should submit official UPI Study transcripts through TESU's admissions office for credit evaluation. UPI Study is listed in TESU's official approved provider directory: TESU Approved Credit Provider Directory
