# Residential Psychology B.S. pathway

Added a separately sourced Liberty residential Psychology B.S. requirement model for the 2026–27 entering catalog. It is a proposed plan with school-confirmation items, not an individual transfer approval or university-wide coverage claim.

The exact 120-credit baseline takes INFT 110 for three technology credits: 37 unique general-education credits, 12 major-foundation credits, 39 major-course credits and 32 free-elective credits. BIOL 102 and PSYC 101 also fulfill the six-credit natural/social-science portion of general education and are counted once. The nested general-education section shows those shared requirements without duplicating degree credit.

The source permits a zero-credit technology assessment case, but this baseline takes the published positive-credit course. The four religion-course waivers remain unclaimed until individual evaluation. The residential culminating selection is PSYC 497; the online PSYC 491 capstone is not substituted. Research and internship alternatives are retained as published choices requiring their own approved credit allocation.

Rules retain 30 Liberty credits, 30 upper-level credits, a C minimum in upper-level major courses and 25% of the 51-credit major through Liberty. The course sequence uses published prerequisite alternatives already included in this ledger. CSER, GPA, graduation application, topic availability and individual evaluation remain explicit checks.

The importer reuses the separately reviewed residential receiving-school equivalency guides through `supplementModel`, preserving their November 10 review dates and exact course identities. Added curriculum facts are dated November 16. A named receiving award still requires the student's current catalog, provider recommendation/version and degree placement evaluation. Unknown outside-course options remain research gaps rather than becoming residency mandates.
