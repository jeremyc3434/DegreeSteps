# TESU Accounting course plan

The additional TESU template is the current **B.S.B.A. in Accounting**, distinct from the university's Accounting/CPA degree. Its 120-credit ledger contains 45 general-education credits, 42 Professional Business Requirements, 18 Accounting credits and 15 free electives. Every slot has a named university course. General-education and business-core course identities are independently corroborated against the current Accounting page, then reused from the reviewed TESU source data.

The required Accounting courses are Intermediate Accounting I and II. This baseline selects Cost Accounting, Advanced Accounting I and II, and Federal Income Taxation from the published four-of-five additional Accounting menu. Auditing remains a published alternative rather than an extra requirement. Formal prerequisites and advisory knowledge are kept separate. The upper-level checks require 12 Accounting credits and six business-core credits, including the capstone, so upper-level free electives cannot incorrectly replace the additional upper-level core requirement.

The standard nonmilitary path retains 15 eligible TESU course-residency credits. Its 105-credit outside bound is calculated from that residency choice; the combined 90-credit noncollegiate ceiling remains separate. SOS-1100 and the capstone are university-only. Military residency, foreign-credit rules and Edison Accelerate require their own scenarios. C minima remain scoped to composition, the business core and Accounting. No state CPA eligibility decision is made.

The fresh-course ledger is fully specified. TESU evaluates the age of existing Accounting and capstone courses against the student's most recent application date: no more than nine Accounting credits may be older than ten years, and the capstone has a five-year limit. Imported earned courses in those groups receive a precise personal audit task until an exact university evaluation establishes their applicability. The app does not substitute today's date for the application date or reject all older Accounting credits. New courses do not receive this task.

Transfer choices come from the existing exact TESU receiving-code evidence through the shared compiler. Their evidence dates, credit quantities, provider conditions and personal-approval distinctions remain intact. This release does not establish new provider equivalencies, turn the separate Study.com Accounting/CPA title table into a BSBA map, or guarantee a specific maximum-transfer basket. Named university coursework fills the researched course ledger where an outside award is not established.

The live current catalog exposes no academic-year edition label. The model therefore uses a source-bound, dated current-catalog identity rather than inventing a 2026–2027 edition. Tuition has its own independently dated source binding. Archived screenshots or provider equivalency years do not establish the curriculum's catalog year.

The offline importer consumes [the reviewed facts](../content/curricula/snapshots/tesu/accounting-current-2026-11-18-reviewed.json) and generates the source model. Production compilation supplies course alternatives and the public course index. The importer has a read-only `--check` mode; local generation never updates review dates automatically.
