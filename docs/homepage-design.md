# A human first impression

The public homepage leads with “Your degree. Within reach.” Students can recognize the concern they arrived with—cost, time, unfinished college, or keeping campus life—and see a short explanation of a possible way forward. A readable three-stage route explains the mechanism: use existing learning, take affordable outside courses where accepted, and finish the remaining university work.

The founder’s reported family experience supplies the concrete story: three years of credit transferred to Liberty, leaving one year on campus. The founder’s own debt-free bachelor’s, MBA and doctorate are attributed personal experience. These are not generalized completion or savings promises. Photography is illustrative; no pictured person is identified as a DegreePath customer or the founder’s family. Provenance and free-use license sources are in image-credits.md.

Real photography, blue/ink typography, warm cream/yellow accents and larger readable controls replace the earlier miniature worksheet hero. Resource pages retain their detailed research and search metadata. All public pages link to the dedicated builder, and the homepage offers useful next steps without requiring transcripts or an account.

The four barrier choices are anchors before enhancement. With JavaScript they become keyboard-operable tabs with one visible panel. Without JavaScript every explanation remains readable and the anchors navigate to the relevant section. Native FAQ and navigation disclosures work without scripts. Reduced motion, 320px layouts, visible focus, image decoding and the real portal handoff are covered by browser journeys.

Photos are self-hosted WebP. The hero has a smaller responsive source and high fetch priority; downstream photos load lazily. The Worker preserves exact image bytes and supports HEAD/ETag checks. No new AI service, tracking service, private-data upload, or authentication change is part of this design release.
