# Automatic route comparison and remaining costs

The student can compare supported degree routes using the same saved courses and prioritize cost, speed or course fit. Comparison cards show remaining university work and scoped cost estimates. Choosing a route previews the first three tasks from the existing checklist, including applicable military and campus tasks.

## Cost behavior

- Remaining tuition and outside course costs are itemized. Provider memberships and bundled exams are counted once per applicable study window or source purchase.
- Completed university and outside coursework is excluded from future charges. Original source records and evaluations stay intact.
- Missing prices never appear as a free degree. A tuition-and-courses estimate remains separate from a fee-inclusive total, with sources, assumptions and exclusions available in the breakdown.
- An unknown cost or time estimate on another eligible route prevents a cheapest/fastest winner badge. Partial base-price suggestions are labeled suggestions and disclose unconfirmed charges.
- The comparison selector overrides earlier setup priorities and changes the automatic shortlist when eligible estimates are comparable. Otherwise the engine explains its course-fit fallback.
