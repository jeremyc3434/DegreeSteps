# TESU remaining-cost planning ranges

The six existing TESU models now have a separate, dated price binding for the standard out-of-state, nonmilitary undergraduate route. The estimate uses the actual selected university coursework remaining in the student's plan. It does not assume the 15-credit residency minimum is the number of university credits left.

Primary TESU pages retrieved on November 25, 2026 establish $584 per credit below nine credits, $4,652 for a term of nine through eighteen eligible credits, and the $50 application plus $98 graduation-audit fee. Hashed text captures and response metadata are retained under `content/estimates/snapshots` and `content/estimates/tesu-rate-bindings-reviewed-2026-11-25.json`. These tuition rates became effective in August 2026. Unrelated provider source review dates are preserved.

The displayed range explicitly identifies out-of-state, nonmilitary tuition. New Jersey/Pennsylvania resident, military, corporate-partner, alumni and other eligibility-specific rates or waivers are not selected. Existing manual personal price inputs remain unchanged; this separately labeled range uses the reviewed published-rate scenario. This binding prices TESU online, Guided Study, Accelerated and e-Pack courses; it does not substitute TECEP or PLA registrations. An Edison Accelerate waiver or different military residency route cannot inherit this rate scenario.

The default nine to fifteen credits per term is an assumption, including a final per-credit term when below nine. Prerequisites, whole-course scheduling and availability may add billed terms. No elapsed graduation date is inferred. The range includes selected provider charges supported by existing dated rates; applicable tax, books, transcript charges and other exclusions stay visible. Reported or finished courses are not purchased again, while individual school acceptance remains a separate matter. Mandatory one-time fees remain included once, with the existing instruction to subtract already-paid charges.

This work does not promote a TESU course match, remove provider approval conditions or increase the count of academically reviewed plans. Price readiness and academic readiness remain independent.

## Suggested course order

The existing planner now keeps currently ready Sophia, Study.com or StraighterLine courses together in its suggested work order rather than alternating paid memberships by worksheet row. An in-progress course takes priority when its prerequisites and completed-credit minimum are met. The suggestion continues other ready work in that membership before opening a different provider. These are suggestions, not billed-month or duration guarantees.

University prerequisites govern university enrollment; only a provider's own published prerequisites are imposed on its outside course. A blocked course stays visible, does not satisfy later prerequisites in the suggested sequence and does not generate a Start action. Completed courses are omitted. Academic worksheet order and selections, private course records, manual checklist dates and notes are unchanged. No new custom-order API or calendar behavior is introduced.
