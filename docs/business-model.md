# DegreeSteps business model and growth plan (draft, 2026-11-22)

## What we sell, and to whom

**Who:** adults who want a degree for less money and time. Typical students:
- working adults with some college credit;
- military members and veterans;
- career changers;
- people stacking low-cost courses (Sophia, Study.com, StraighterLine, CLEP).

**The promise:** "See exactly which cheap courses count toward a real degree, what's left, and what it costs — from the school's own published rules."

**Why it's defensible:** the plans are researched from source, checked, and kept current through the Pathway Factory, and every claim links to a school document. Forums and affiliate blogs don't have this.

**The one rule that protects the business:** trust. Money never changes which plans, schools or courses a student sees, or their order. Every paid relationship is labelled and explained on `/why-its-free/`. A core test enforces that plan building and ranking never read partner settings.

## Where demand comes from

1. **Search is the main engine.** Students search exact questions:
   - "Does Sophia Intro to Psychology transfer to WGU?"
   - "Cheapest online psychology degree"
   - "Liberty online criminal justice transfer credits"

   We already publish 272 indexed pages (plan pages, a 183-course transfer directory, school and provider pages) with structured data. Next:
   - school-by-provider transfer guides, such as "Every Sophia course that counts at Excelsior";
   - "cheapest and fastest" comparison pages built only from our data;
   - a fresh "last checked" date on every page.
2. **Tools as magnets.** "What are my credits worth?" and "Help me choose" give an instant, personal answer. These are the pages to share and to link from forums.
3. **Communities.** The people we serve already gather in r/WGU, r/CLEP, r/distancelearning, veteran groups and degree-hacking Discords. Answer questions there with links to the exact plan or transfer page, and never spam.
4. **Sharing.** "Share this plan" links let a student send their plan to a spouse, a school advisor or a friend.
5. **Partners.** Military education offices, community-college transfer advisors and employer HR teams can point people to DegreeSteps once it has proven useful.
6. **The AI coach, later.** It answers questions from our data, and its anonymous question log tells us which schools and degrees to research next.

## Conversion: the steps that matter

Visit → **see a plan** (finder, guided or plan page) → **build my plan** (worksheet) → **save it** (account) → **act**: click through to a provider course, email the school, or ask the school to contact me.

| Step | What to watch | First target to beat |
|---|---|---|
| Visit → plan seen | share of visits that open a plan | 35% |
| Plan seen → built | share of plan viewers who open the worksheet | 30% |
| Built → saved | share of builders who save to an account | 20% |
| Built → acted | provider clicks or school emails per plan built | 0.5 |

These targets are starting guesses. Replace them with real numbers after two weeks of measurement.

The measurement is anonymous, daily aggregate counts only, with no cookies, IP addresses or third-party trackers. It appears in the owner's Growth panel on the admin page.

## The next 90 days

**Weeks 1–2: measure and get ready**
- Funnel measurement and the owner Growth panel (being built now).
- One partner-link layer for every provider link. Off until a partnership exists, so nothing changes for students.
- The `/why-its-free/` disclosure page, for you to review.
- You apply to the provider affiliate or partner programs.

**Weeks 3–6: grow and convert**
- Search pages: per-school transfer guides and "cheapest path" comparisons.
- Share-a-plan links.
- Clearer save prompts after a plan is built.
- Turn on the AI coach (capped at $3/day).
- A weekly 10-minute forum habit, answering real questions with exact links.

**Weeks 7–12: first revenue**
- Switch on approved provider partner links (labelled).
- If you choose it, launch opt-in "Ask this school to contact me" with one or two schools.
- Test the paid Plan review with a simple checkout. Pick the payment provider when we get there.

**Measures of success at day 90:**
- organic visits growing week over week;
- at least 30% of plan viewers building a plan;
- first referral revenue;
- a clear signal on whether students will pay for review.

## Decisions only the owner can make

1. **School inquiries:** yes or no to opt-in school inquiries (#2). It is the biggest revenue line, but it needs care to keep trust.
2. **Paid Plan review:** yes or no, and who would do the reviews.
3. **Affiliate programs:** which ones to apply to (all need your identity and payout details).
4. **Email:** whether to add an email service for "plan alerts" and saved-plan reminders. It needs a provider account and an unsubscribe flow.
