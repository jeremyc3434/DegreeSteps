# UMPI BLS Management route: current evidence and coverage gap

The existing `umpi-bls-management` route has no verified course-level degree model. Do not describe it as an executable transfer plan or silently substitute a different degree. Keep the student's exact goal and course records, explain the missing catalog-specific map, and provide a direct return to an available working plan.

## Verified primary evidence

- [UMPI General Education Curriculum](https://www.umpi.edu/academics/general-education-curriculum/) says a new curriculum begins September 1, 2026, organized around eight General Learning Outcomes. Students newly accepted for the 2026–27 academic year or later follow the new curriculum; continuing students retain their incoming catalog requirements. An old general-education recipe cannot establish a new student's current placements.
- [UMPI's YourPace Liberal Studies page](https://www.umpi.edu/yourpace/academic_programs/liberal-studies/) still lists a Bachelor of Liberal Studies with a Management Minor. This supports retaining the named goal for investigation. The retrieved page does not supply the exact current Management minor course requirements or resolve its catalog-specific availability.
- That marketing page contains other minor-specific totals and text. Those figures must not be copied into the Management minor merely because the degree family is the same.

## Unresolved catalog evidence

The academic catalog returned HTTP 403 through the research tool at these published links:

- [YourPace program catalog](https://catalog.umpi.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=12&poid=1813&returnto=621)
- [YourPace course search](https://catalog.umpi.edu/content.php?catoid=12&navoid=620)
- [General-education catalog link](https://catalog.umpi.edu/preview_program.php?catoid=12&poid=1783)

UMPI also links a [new general-education student document](https://www.umpi.edu/academics/wp-content/uploads/sites/19/2026/06/GenEd_Student_OnePage.docx). The web research tool did not extract that DOCX. Its detailed category requirements were therefore not verified in this review.

Community discussions report changes to Management and other minors in fall 2026. Those reports conflict with the still-published marketing listing and do not establish that the student's exact program is available or retired. Do not label it discontinued without an authoritative catalog or university statement.

## What is required before publishing a degree map

Obtain the applicable catalog and admission cohort, the exact Management minor course list and current availability, the new or grandfathered general-education requirements, and the BLS rules for separate upper-level electives and institutional/minor residency. Verify receiving awards and their applicability to those particular requirements before proposing an outside-course basket. Provider acceptance or an old receiving equivalency alone does not establish placement in the new general-education curriculum.

No UMPI JSON degree model was created from the incomplete evidence. This is a coverage limitation, not a finding that the student's courses will be rejected or that their goal is impossible.
