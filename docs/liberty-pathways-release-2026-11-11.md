# Liberty pathway evidence and academic rules

This release strengthens the ten newer Liberty curricula and three established Liberty models. The supplement adds 352 directly reviewed options to 122 named requirements in the newer curricula. Across the source document and three established model files it records 525 placements, 101 original provider-course identities and 41 receiving-course codes. Repeated options across degrees are not advertised as distinct courses.

Mappings come from Liberty's delivery-specific Sophia, Study.com and StraighterLine guides and its CLEP table. Guide publication dates remain separate from catalog years and the November 10 review date. Named receiving awards are matched by code and credits; generic elective awards are not promoted to a required major course by similar titles. Exact additions include Study.com Finance 301 to BUSI 320 and Psychology major courses. The Finance title difference remains an explicit condition.

The planner now shares one major-residency calculation between search and validation: the stronger of the explicit minimum and the published fraction times its reviewed credit basis. Incomplete or malformed policies cannot silently become zero requirements. All 51 major credits transferred into the online Business model correctly fail major residency even if overall university residency is met.

Sourced grade minima follow each program's actual scope. A mixed major-elective C rule applies when the receiving award is upper level; the original source course's level alone does not establish that. Grades D/C-minus and CLEP scores below 50 do not qualify for requirements needing C/50. Records remain saved. Changing a recorded grade or score clears prior evaluations rather than retaining approval for changed facts. Optional grade and score fields are available in the existing course-record form.

The online Business baseline retains three UNIV 104 credits and 39 elective credits, totaling 120 unique credits. Its published zero-credit possibility still requires individual confirmation. CLEP's receiving credits are preserved; independent source credit quantities remain unknown where the receiving guide does not establish them. SOPH-0009 is Visual Communications.

The source supplement is reproducible after the importer and has a read-only check in the production build. The compiled registry and course index were regenerated. Published mappings remain proposed planning evidence, not individual school awards, confirmed provider recommendation windows, guaranteed prices or promised completion times.

Final validation: ten regression suites passed, including real catalog-to-Sheet-to-Guide-to-Journey grade/residency cases, conditional upper-level grades, exam scores, duplicate/source preservation, full curriculum acceptance and checklist behavior. The production build, asset routes, API boundary, JavaScript syntax and whitespace checks passed. Earlier mathematical optimizer and planner suites passed; the Liberty waiver fixture was updated for newly available mappings while retaining the university-approval blocker.
