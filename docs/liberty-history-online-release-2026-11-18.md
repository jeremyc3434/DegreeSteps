# Liberty History online course plan

The new 2026–27 B.S. History online template accounts for all 120 credits: 36 unique general-education credits, six major foundations, 36 history major credits and 42 free electives. The [official degree plan](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/colleges-schools/arts-sciences/history-ba-bs/history-bs-online/) explicitly places HIUS 221 and GEOG 200 in the two information-literacy positions; each course is counted once, in major foundations. The three-credit UNIV 104 case and both conditional religion-waiver requirements remain in the baseline.

Eight named, online-offered upper-level courses fill the published elective blocks: six European-history credits, six U.S.-history credits, three world-history credits and nine additional history credits. Together with HIST 300 and HIST 491, they supply all 30 required upper-level credits. These are proposed choices within the published restrictions, rather than newly invented mandatory courses. No residential-only elective is substituted into the online plan. The current [European](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/courses/hieu/), [U.S.](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/courses/hius/) and [world-history](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/courses/hiwd/) course lists establish the selected courses and delivery formats.

The [HIST course catalog](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/courses/hist/) requires senior status, HIST 300 and one permitted upper-level history course before the online capstone. The [online academic policy](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/academic-support/academic-information-policies-online-program/) defines senior classification at 72 earned semester credits; the plan preserves that threshold. This baseline retains HIST 300 and HIEU 322 as its prerequisite sequence. It does not turn the entire published list of alternatives into requirements.

The degree retains 30 Liberty credits, 30 upper-level credits, a C minimum in upper-level major coursework and the published 25% major-residency rule. The major basis is the complete 42-credit Major Requirements ledger, including foundations; credits shared with general education are not counted a second time in the degree total. Current outside-course options are supplied separately by the existing exact school/format award compiler, including reviewed history receiving awards and eligible free electives. A school's overall 90-credit outside ceiling does not mean a particular 90-credit basket is feasible or approved.

The selected course plan has a complete ledger, but it is not an individual transfer award or a teacher-licensure program. Prices and provider credit versions remain attached to their own reviewed evidence rather than inferred from course titles.

The 42-credit free-elective allowance also has fourteen distinct, named online university fallback courses. A student who deselects all outside providers therefore retains a complete course plan rather than fourteen generic "choose a course" placeholders. These are editable selections within unrestricted elective capacity; their codes do not become mandatory receiving requirements, and the existing outside-course choices keep their actual receiving identities. The stable `free-electives-1` through `free-electives-14` IDs and original 120-credit ledger are preserved. Current official course descriptions establish the selected courses' three-credit quantities, online delivery and absence of formal prerequisites. Residential-only BUSI 105 and BUSI 240 are excluded.

The broader Interdisciplinary Studies B.S. was also investigated. Its current parent page describes 51 major credits including RSCH 201, while its current online degree-completion page describes 48 without that course. No new Interdisciplinary Studies bachelor template was created from those conflicting totals.

Verification covers shared-credit allocation, department/level restrictions, source bindings, conservative waiver cases, determinism, actual compiled outside options, residency, upper-level totals and capstone prerequisites. This work does not claim a browser-rendering pass or universal Liberty program coverage.
