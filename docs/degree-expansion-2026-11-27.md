## Evidence and academic safeguards

- **Liberty:** Each cognate has its own retained 2026–2027 degree plan and current online course descriptions. The ledger retains UNIV 104 and does not assume religion or technology waivers. Criminal and Developmental Psychology use 18 free-elective credits within their printed 12–21 range; the other three use 24 within 18–27. Major/cognate residency denominators are 66 or 60 credits as applicable. CJUS 230 is counted once as a Criminal Psychology elective to satisfy the CJUS 340 prerequisite. Residential-only PSYC 361 is excluded from the online baseline. These psychology pathways do not establish counseling or psychology licensure eligibility.
- **Liberty placement integrity:** A reusable institution-wide receiving award is separate from permission to use it in a particular degree. Every new placement binds the exact new model, catalog, modality, requirement and degree source. New degrees do not inherit the older shared bundle's explicit program approval. Published electives retain independently reviewed provider versions and separate official Liberty receiving-guide evidence. Unreviewed CJUS 200 and PSYC 235 alternatives remain conditional exact-course options; they are not relabeled as free electives to pass the checks. Existing Liberty guards remain in place.
- **TESU:** Both degrees use the exact 45 general education + 42 professional business + 18 area + 15 free-elective structure. Shared business blocks and school awards are reused, while the six area courses, formal prerequisite closure and upper/lower division remain degree-specific. CIS is a **B.S.**, and COS 1160/COS 2410 remain lower division. COS 2410's C++ preparation advisory is retained rather than replaced by an invented Java equivalency or prerequisite waiver.
- **APU:** The official APU transfer finder, current exact degrees and course descriptions support the three 60-credit ledgers. Each starts with 30 published Sophia credits and 30 APU credits. The overall transfer limit is 45, the distinct nontraditional limit is 30, and minimum institutional residency is 15. The 30-credit alternative cap does not overwrite bachelor policy. Fourteen original provider identities have separate ACE recommendation and receiving-school effective windows; every reviewed source window is contained within its receiving window. Awards are scoped to **American Public University**, not automatically to AMU, Rasmussen or Hondros.
- **Phoenix:** Current catalog pages distinguish semester credits from competency units, exact admissions, capstone prerequisites and pricing. MBA/MIS admissions preserve the catalog's three-year related-degree and five-year other-degree experience alternatives. MHA retains healthcare leadership and workplace-access requirements. The three CB programs cost $300 per credit; they are not unlimited subscriptions. Direct Assessment Psychology costs $2,749 per 12-week term, permits at most four three-unit courses per term, and retains its annual unit cap. It is non-licensure and not eligible for Title IV federal aid. Individual admission and state availability are not assumed.

New source captures retain actual retrieval metadata and content hashes. Reused evidence keeps its original retrieval/review provenance; a new degree review is not represented as a fresh retrieval of every shared source. No personal transcript awards were created.

## Preservation and scalability

The prior 45 ready model IDs are retained. The three original APU open-slot program inputs remain byte-identical to the previous release. The new named APU baselines have distinct `reviewed-2026` model and route IDs, so older students retain their saved course choices, notes, course records and selected routes without an automatic migration.

This cohort exercises the scalable authoring approach: shared school policy, receiving awards, original provider identities and common curriculum blocks are reused; a new degree supplies its own catalog identity, requirement ledger, component allocations and academic differences. Liberty's five cognates share a reviewed psychology backbone. TESU's two areas share the reviewed business structure. APU's three ledgers share independently versioned Sophia identities. Each new degree still receives a fresh placement proof and runs the existing academic gates. Programs publish separately rather than enlarging one fallback payload.

The next expansion should reuse these school families while concentrating research on missing upper-level/major matches and new exact provider versions. More templates alone do not establish maximum transfer optimization: the current 60–69 Liberty and 63–66 TESU outside-credit plans have room for further source-backed improvements where school rules permit.

## Holds and remaining limits

TESU Operations Management remains excluded: the OPM 4200 course description formally requires OPM 4110, while the degree lists APS 4020. No authoritative course recode or waiver was established; a title resemblance is not enough. Other candidate programs with unresolved catalog identity, prerequisites or mappings remain research work rather than ready plans.

Additional conditional providers, institutional course alternatives and future recommendation versions are not promoted solely to increase the plan count. Official transcript evaluation, actual minimum grades, personal admission eligibility and future section availability remain individual university decisions. Cost and timing outputs remain scenarios with their stated coverage; this release does not promise a complete all-in estimate or guaranteed graduation date.
