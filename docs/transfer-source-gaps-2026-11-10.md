Those source credits remain separate from receiving competency units, particularly the two 2-CU Business Environment requirements. The ledger moves from 12 to 22 Business Management requirements with at least one conditional outside option (38 to 66 receiving CUs represented); the ten new candidates represent 28 receiving CUs. These figures describe evidence coverage, not approved transferable totals.

The Communications 301 page is internally inconsistent: its heading calls the credit lower-level while its credit section calls it upper-division. The option records neither source nor receiving level and explicitly requires confirmation. A course number is not treated as proof of level.

**Finance was deliberately excluded.** Study.com's table proposes Finance 101: Principles of Finance for Introduction to Business Finance, but the university's current D775 guideline excludes general finance. That conflict needs a current WGU partner articulation or individual university decision; the university requirement remains without an added option.

## Computer Science: unresolved catalog gap

The retained official [BSCS guide](https://www.wgu.edu/content/dam/wgu-65-assets/western-governors/documents/program-guides/information-technology/BSCS.pdf) model is **202611 / 121 CUs**. The [live Study.com Computer Science table](https://study.com/college/western-governors-university/degrees/computer-science-transfer-credits-requirements-degree-plan.html) does not state a catalog, still describes 117 CUs, and includes older receiving titles alongside changed rows. For example, its current CS109 row says Scripting and Programming — Applications, whereas the previous retained table said Foundations. Matching a subset of titles cannot establish that the table applies to 202611.

Potentially useful unresolved candidates include Math 104 / Calculus I, CS204 / Data Management — Applications, CS202 / Fundamentals of Information Security, CS108 / Network and Security — Foundations, CS109 / Scripting and Programming — Applications, and Philosophy 104 / Ethics in Technology. They were **not** added. The [WGU CS pathway URL examined](https://partners.wgu.edu/transfer-pathway-agreement?uniqueId=BSCS7110&collegeCode=IT&instId=796&programId=261) also returned no readable lines and does not itself establish its applicability to 202611.

The [Study.com policy update dated September 15, 2026](https://study.com/college/credit-transfer/understanding-wgu-2026-transfer-credit-updates.html) says BSCS Data Structures and Algorithms I, Discrete Mathematics I, and Computer Architecture must be completed at WGU. Historical equivalents for these requirements must not be imported. The article directs readers to WGU's program-specific partner table for current decisions. It also says business degrees are unaffected by that particular 2026 policy update; that broad statement does not certify any individual Business Management equivalency.
