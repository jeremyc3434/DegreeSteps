# Healthcare Administration online curriculum

The new model is Liberty's **Healthcare Administration (B.S.) — Online**, for the published fall 2026–summer 2027 catalog cohort. Its exact parent directory entry now has a distinct online route.

The [official program requirements](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/colleges-schools/business/healthcare-administration-major-bs/healthcare-administration-online/) publish 39–42 general-education credits, nine major-foundation credits, 57 major-core credits, 12–24 free electives and a 120-credit degree. The model chooses a conservative valid allocation: 39 credits of proposed approved general education, three UNIV 104 credits, all nine foundations, all 57 major-core credits and 12 free electives. These sum to 120 without any duplicate allocation.

The [reviewed online general-education list](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/colleges-schools/applied-studies-academic-success/general-education-courses-online-programs/) supplies the proposed elective choices. Those choices deliberately differ from the three major-foundation courses; the catalog permits foundation overlap, but this baseline does not rely on it. The approved-list evidence retains its original November 10 check date. The program's full native-web extract was read on November 16; a subsequent refresh timed out.

UNIV 104 is printed as zero to three credits. The baseline retains three and does not assert an independently proven university-only restriction. A confirmed zero-credit determination can release three credits for additional electives, within the published range. RLGN 104 and THEO 104 remain in place until any individual transfer waiver is approved.

The published requirements enforce 30 Liberty credits, 30 upper-level credits and 25% of the 66-credit major through Liberty. The C minimum applies to upper-level major courses. It is not copied onto lower-level foundations or every general-education row. An internship is recommended rather than invented as a separate compulsory course.

The offline importer reproduces the source pack from the reviewed fact snapshot. The common compiler can attach separately sourced same-school receiving-course choices, preserving their evidence conditions. A complete requirement ledger does not establish individual transfer approval, professional licensure eligibility or a guaranteed accelerated schedule.

Run `python scripts/import-liberty-online-expansion.py --check`, then regenerate the registry and run `node --test liberty-online-expansion.test.cjs`.
