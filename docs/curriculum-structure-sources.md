# Curriculum structure review

Checked November 3, 2026. This is presentation metadata for the four existing requirement ledgers, not new course equivalencies or additional credit awards. `scripts/add-curriculum-structure.mjs` applies the reviewed hierarchy without changing requirement IDs, placements, grades, course options or credit budgets.

Liberty's 2026–27 [residential business](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/colleges-schools/business/business-administration-data-analysis-major-bs/business-administration-bs-interdisciplinary-business-resident/), [residential Criminal Justice](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/colleges-schools/helms-government/criminal-justice-major-bs/criminal-justice-resident/) and [online Criminal Justice](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/colleges-schools/helms-government/criminal-justice-major-bs/criminal-justice-online/) plans name six general-education sections:

- Communication & Information Literacy
- Technological Solutions & Quantitative Reasoning
- Critical Thinking
- Civic & Global Engagement
- Social & Scientific Inquiry
- Christianity & Contexts

The [residential](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/colleges-schools/applied-studies-academic-success/general-education-courses-residential-programs/) and [online](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/colleges-schools/applied-studies-academic-success/general-education-courses-online-programs/) approved lists establish which selected courses serve those areas. Business additionally has Major Foundational Courses (18), Major Courses (36), and an Interdisciplinary Business Cognate (12). Criminal Justice has Major Foundational Courses (6) and Major Courses (39). Capstones remain in their actual official section; their credits are not added again.

Liberty business shares BUSI 105 with communication, BUSI 205 with critical thinking and BUSI 240 with social inquiry. BUSI 201 meets a **zero-credit technology competency**; its three major credits do not create three more general-education hours. Both Criminal Justice plans share GOVT 220 with civic/global requirements. Online Criminal Justice therefore has a real civic/global subsection with zero uniquely allocated credits and three shared credits. The subsection stays visible.

The two residential degree pages list Christian service (CSER) among graduation requirements. It appears separately as a non-credit requirement needing individual school confirmation. The online model does not acquire CSER from the residential policy. An uploaded individual audit is a design reference, not a source for universal waivers or course awards.

[TESU's current General Management catalog](https://tesu.smartcatalogiq.com/en/current/undergraduate-catalog/degree-programs-and-certificates/bachelor-of-science-in-business-administration/bs-in-general-management) has General Education (45), Professional Business Requirements (42), Area of Study (18), and Electives (15). Its general-education divisions are Intellectual and Practical Skills (15), Civic and Global Leadership (9), Knowledge of Human Cultures (15), and Scientific Knowledge (6). The [approved general-education category page](https://www.tesu.edu/degrees-programs/courses/general-education/2021-and-after.php) supports their nested communication, quantitative, information, diversity, ethics, civic, humanities and social-science headings. The existing selected Area of Study has Accounting, Finance and Management at six credits each. The capstone is already inside the 42 professional-business credits.

The model distinguishes unique allocations from requirement fulfillment. Parent and child fulfillment bars may include the same shared course; their labels disclose shared hours. The overall degree bar and source enrollment records count it once. A zero-credit competency has a visible reference and status without invented credit hours. Approved waiver scenarios carry their own applicable hierarchy and never create completed coursework. Missing or conflicting hierarchy metadata retains visible fallback requirements rather than dropping rows or fabricating categories.
