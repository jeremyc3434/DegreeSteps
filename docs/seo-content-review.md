# Public educational content review

Reviewed November 1, 2026. The first editorial collection is `content/public-content.json`: eight guides and six articles, totaling 8,044 words. Each piece has a distinct student question, a description, category, estimated reading time, editorial review date, substantive sections, primary-source links, and related-content slugs. Guide routes are `/guides/<slug>/`; article routes are `/blog/<slug>/`. The collection does not invent earlier publication dates, named credentialed reviewers, testimonials, rankings, conversion statistics, or search-traffic results.

## Editorial scope

The guides cover transfer fundamentals, ACE/NCCRS and accreditation, credit-source comparison, residential transfer, degree audits and what-if plans, military learning, cost and pace, and learning-record preparation. Articles answer narrower decisions: interpreting a transfer ceiling, outside work before enrollment, buying subscriptions, changing majors, interpreting community outcomes, and graduate acceleration.

The content leads with actionable student decisions. Spreadsheet and hypothetical degree examples are labeled illustrative or hypothetical and do not imply a real school's approval. A higher transfer ceiling does not become applied credit. Original units, completed learning, proposed placements, and individual school confirmations stay separate. Community votes and reports do not award credit or establish professional eligibility. The two partial application course maps are not described as complete audits for every available school.

Provider pricing is deliberately left to the dated source directory and current provider pages. Editorial articles explain cost structure and comparison rather than duplicating volatile price tables. Study.com and StraighterLine assessment formats are distinguished using the current official support sources already reviewed in the source catalog. Neither a broad provider relationship nor a preparation product creates an automatic degree placement.

The founder's degree and debt experience appears once in the graduate article as a founder-reported account supplied by the user. It is not described as independently audited or as a prediction for other students.

## Source review

The collection uses existing reviewed primary sources from the application's catalogs and additional official search/open review in this session:

- ACE learner, recommendation, and transcript guidance: recommendations and records are distinct from the receiving school's award.
- NCCRS student FAQ: recommendations become actual academic awards through colleges. The official public URL intermittently failed direct retrieval; current official search material supported the narrow distinction. The existing research also preserves the accessible official NYSED-hosted FAQ.
- U.S. Department of Education accreditation guidance: institutional and programmatic accreditation are separate questions, and accreditation does not guarantee transfer.
- College Board CLEP policy guidance: exam acceptance, required results, degree applicability, and duplicate learning must be checked with the institution.
- Modern States official FAQ and support: preparation/vouchers do not create a separate academic credit award. Reimbursement and delivery details are left for a current check rather than promising that every testing cost is covered.
- Liberty residential policy, transfer resources, registrar guidance, and current academic catalog: residential transfer planning uses residential program evidence and preserves course-specific evaluation.
- WGU transfer and transcript-exclusion guidance: prestart record/evaluation rules matter. The content does not turn one school's deadlines into a universal rule or repeat a possibly conflicting day-of-month cutoff.
- DANTES, VA, and current Air University registrar material: military learning needs an individual school evaluation, and current consolidated AU/CCAF record instructions supersede conflicting older pages.
- Purdue Global ExcelTrack, MBA, and tuition material; South College graduate CBE and DBA pages: graduate acceleration is program-specific. Purdue Global's explicit ExcelTrack VA/Federal TA exclusion is attributed to its current tuition information rather than generalized to all programs.

No long quotations or copied source passages are used. The explanation, comparisons, hypothetical scenarios, and planning suggestions are original editorial synthesis. Every source is linked directly to the official page rather than a search result or community assertion.

## Maintenance

Keep the content's editorial review date separate from individual source and policy effective dates. Recheck provider format references, transcript procedures, graduate funding exclusions, and institutional transfer rules during the existing monthly review. A policy change should update the affected article and its source links; it should not silently confer a new approval on an older saved plan.

The corpus supports discovery and student education. Search indexing, rankings, domain verification, analytics, and ongoing publishing are separate implementation and operating activities; this content does not claim those outcomes have occurred.
