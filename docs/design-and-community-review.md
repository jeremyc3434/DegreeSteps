# A simpler degree workspace, improved by real outcomes

Reviewed November 1, 2026. Research covers the products' public pages and design material, not an authenticated audit of their private workspaces. Muse and Grok Bot are the inferred matches to the user's names.

## Design references

- [Muse's design essay](https://introducing.muse.ai/) favors a clear main workspace, richer artifacts for structured tasks, optional secondary conversations, and detail revealed when it matters. DegreePath keeps the actual degree worksheet central; it does not add a paid AI conversation to replace precise course controls.
- [Grok Bot's public product page](https://x.ai/bot) emphasizes work completed and repeatable responsibilities. DegreePath should make the next useful decision visible and show what changed, while keeping policy research and private records out of repetitive setup.
- [ChatGPT dots](https://chatgpt.com/features/dots/) presents ongoing responsibilities, progress and reviewable decisions. DegreePath applies that idea through inspectable course choices, student outcomes, and a concise review action. The app does not claim to autonomously enroll students or update school policies.
- [Tesla's ordering guide](https://www.tesla.com/support/ordering-new-vehicle) separates choosing/configuring a vehicle from later delivery tasks. DegreePath similarly lets a student choose a degree, bring existing credit and review the resulting plan without completing every optional setting first.
- The engineering sequence commonly called the algorithm is documented in the original [Everyday Astronaut interview write-up](https://everydayastronaut.com/starbase-tour-and-interview-with-elon-musk/): question requirements, remove unnecessary parts, simplify, accelerate, then automate. It is a useful product discipline rather than a reason to remove necessary academic constraints.

## Changes in this release

The main header now concentrates on My credits, Review plan and More. Secondary comparison, export, sharing and research actions sit inside More; the table toolbar keeps search, filtering and Plan settings. Transfer conditions, alternative menus and notes open from their relevant row. Routine notices no longer move the worksheet. Cost and pace remain available with their actual unknowns; no invented completion date or total price replaces a missing quote.

The spreadsheet remains the main plan. A community badge is attached to a particular outside course placement, not a global provider endorsement. Published plans receive evidence for their exact curriculum; changing actual courses invalidates the prior plan badge. Text edits and reordered courses do not erase otherwise applicable evidence.

## Shared curriculum and evidence

Students can copy a published route into their private worksheet, adapt proposed courses, and publish a reviewed public curriculum. The share preview preserves a link to the originating route. This is reusable community curriculum; the release does not assign a software license to the application or retroactively relicense contributor prose.

Outcomes are distinct from popularity. Two different authenticated member accounts must supply recent successful outcomes with public proof reviewed by another moderator for the same exact scope and catalog before Community verified appears. Changed/rejected outcomes and flagged evidence prevent an unsupported verification claim. Reports, flags and review actions are persisted; no sample outcomes or verified badges are preloaded.

Reports feed a human review queue and can identify mappings needing policy research. They do not automatically award a student's credits, remove a school restriction or rewrite the catalog. Official policies, community reports and the individual school's evaluation remain independently inspectable. Private transcripts, account pages, local notes and learning records are excluded from public report payloads.

See [the evidence API contract](community-verification-contract.md) for exact scope, privacy, freshness and moderation rules.
