# TESU Finance course plan

The reviewed current B.S.B.A. in Finance contains 120 semester credits: 45 general education, 42 Professional Business Requirements, 18 Finance, and 15 electives. The Finance courses are FIN-3140, FIN-3340, FIN-3820, FIN-3210, FIN-3310, and FDR-4400, each three upper-level credits. The existing exact TESU receiving-code transfer evidence is reused with its original conditions; no additional provider equivalencies are created.

The 15 elective credits include a fixed three-credit MAT-1290 Precalculus selection and four unrestricted three-credit elective choices. FIN-3210 and FIN-3820 formally require FIN-3010, STA-2010, and MAT-1190 or MAT-1290. This baseline selects MAT-1290, including its MAT-1210 prerequisite. The math row cannot be replaced by an unrelated free elective merely to increase the transfer count. MAT-1190 is an alternative, not another mandatory course. FIN-3140 formally requires FIN-3010; no formal prerequisite block was found for FIN-3340, FIN-3310, or FDR-4400.

Rechecked business-core prerequisites also retain ACC-1010 before ACC-1020 and FIN-3010, MAT-1210 before STA-2010 and BUS-2100, and MAN-2100 before MAN-3720. BUS-2100 permits MAT-1050 as an alternative, but the degree already includes MAT-1210. MAN-3720's formal block prints legacy MAN-210 while its advisory hyperlinks the current MAN-2100 course; that same-school course identity is preserved separately from any outside transfer award.

At least 12 upper-level Finance credits, the capstone, and three additional upper-level AOS/core credits are required. All six selected Finance requirements are upper level; the baseline also retains upper-level FIN-3010 and BUS-4210. An Accounting-specific six-credit core minimum is not imposed on this degree. C minima apply to composition, business core, and Finance. The standard nonmilitary 15-credit course residency, derived 105-credit outside bound, and 90-credit combined noncollegiate cap remain distinct.

Existing Finance credits are subject to the current application-date currency policy: no more than nine of the eighteen area-of-study credits may be older than ten years, and the capstone has a five-year limit. Typed source-bound metadata enables the Journey's personal audit task for affected existing records. This does not perform an automatic application-date calculation or reject every older course. Fresh-course plans require no such personal review task.

The current catalog page exposes no academic-year edition label. Its dated current-catalog identity is retained instead of manufacturing a 2026–2027 edition. Published tuition has an independent dated source binding; books, provider charges, and fees are separate from tuition estimates. Military residency, foreign-credit rules, and Edison Accelerate remain separate scenarios.

The offline importer consumes the reviewed Finance facts and writes the source model, with a read-only `--check` mode. Shared policies retain their original source-review dates. The compiler attaches outside-course choices by reviewed receiving code; course-title similarity is not a mapping.
