Independent original-source evidence identifies SDCM-0025 as Business 103, Introductory Business Law; Business 212 is not that course. The join now checks a declared Study.com course label against the independently established identity and holds a contradiction. It does not change WGU's source or invent a corrected award. The spreadsheet's proposed English 204 alternative is absent from the exact current WGU Software Engineering receiving agreement, so it is not added merely because the spreadsheet lists it. WGU's Geography guideline explicitly permits behavioral/social-science alternatives, including economics; a different subject label alone is not proof that an equivalency is invalid.
