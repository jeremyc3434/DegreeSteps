# Liberty Criminal Justice general-program models

Checked November 3, 2026 against the 2026–27 catalog and Liberty-hosted transfer guides. These models support an actual course ledger for the general Criminal Justice B.S., separately for residential and online study. They do not represent the other Criminal Justice concentrations.

## Authoritative requirements

- [Parent major and delivery choices](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/colleges-schools/helms-government/criminal-justice-major-bs/)
- [Residential general-program requirements](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/colleges-schools/helms-government/criminal-justice-major-bs/criminal-justice-resident/)
- [Online general-program requirements](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/colleges-schools/helms-government/criminal-justice-major-bs/criminal-justice-online/)
- [Approved residential general education](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/colleges-schools/applied-studies-academic-success/general-education-courses-residential-programs/)
- [Approved online general education](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/colleges-schools/applied-studies-academic-success/general-education-courses-online-programs/)

The parent catalog record is an overview offering both delivery formats, not a delivery-specific degree audit. Its previously empty modality metadata must not be interpreted as incompatibility with either online or campus study.

Both models require 120 semester credits, 30 upper-level credits, 30 Liberty credits, 2.0 overall GPA, C or higher in major courses, and the catalog's major residency requirement. Residential students also have noncredit CSER obligations. The major ledger contains six foundation hours (GOVT 200/220) and 39 core hours (CJUS 200/230/231/300/310/320/330/340/350/400/410/420 and GOVT 480).

## Unique-credit accounting

| Baseline | Unique general education | Major/foundation | Free electives | Total |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| Residential | 40 | 45 | 35 | 120 |
| Online | 39 | 45 | 36 | 120 |

GOVT 220 is an approved cultural-studies choice and a named major foundation course. It appears once, in the major ledger, with an explicit cultural-studies overlap. GOVT 200 is not used to replace critical thinking. Approved ENGL 102 and ARTS 209 fill the two information-literacy slots; COMS 101, MATH 115, ETHC 101 and PSYC 101 fill their appropriate categories. Residential natural science uses ENVR 215; online uses BIOL 102 because the current online approved list differs.

Residential accounting assumes the three-credit INFT 110 technology choice, rather than a zero-credit assessment exemption. Online accounting retains three credits for UNIV 104 rather than assuming exemption. Approved exemptions or transfer-triggered religion waivers award no credits and would require additional nonduplicating electives to preserve 120. They are documented for review and are not automatically applied.

The catalog's 25% major residency rule is conservatively modeled as 12 hours, rounding 25% of all 45 foundation/core hours to complete three-credit courses. This is a planning reservation; Liberty's exact major-residency denominator remains an individual verification item. Generated baseline drafts currently reserve more than this minimum.

Free electives use exact published receiving awards. Residential fills its last two hours with the separately published one-credit SOPH-0021 and SOPH-0041 awards, never a fractional allocation from a three-credit course. University alternatives for those generic elective awards still require selecting actual one-credit courses.

## Prerequisites and schedule limits

[CJUS course catalog](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/courses/cjus/) and [GOVT course catalog](https://catalog.liberty.edu/undergraduate/courses/govt/) establish the major sequence:

- CJUS 300/320/330/340/350/420 require CJUS 200 and 230.
- Residential CJUS 310 requires CJUS 200; online requires CJUS 200 and 230.
- CJUS 400/410 permit CJUS 200 plus 230, or GOVT 200 plus 346. The baseline models the CJUS pair; it does not add optional GOVT 346.
- GOVT 220/480 require GOVT 200.
- CJUS 200/230/231 list no prerequisites. These required courses list both delivery formats.
- CJUS 420 has a fall-semester offering note. General-education/elective and provider prerequisites, exact offerings, transcript deadlines and term scheduling still require review.

No completion date or executable final-year schedule is asserted.
